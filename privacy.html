Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Effective immediately

Plain English summary: We collect only what we need to run ReviewBoostPRO. We never sell your data. We never share it with third parties for marketing. Your customers' data belongs to you.

1. Who We Are

ReviewBoostPRO is a software product operated by ReviewBoost Technologies, India. We provide a WhatsApp-based Google review management tool for Indian businesses.

For privacy questions, contact us at support@reviewboost.in

2. What Data We Collect

Account data: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and business name.

Business data: Your Google review link, business profile information, and message templates you create.

Customer data: Names and phone numbers of customers you add to ReviewBoostPRO to send review requests.

Usage data: How you use the dashboard — sends, opens, review redirects, and private feedback received.

Payment data: Handled entirely by Razorpay. We do not store your card or banking details.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the ReviewBoostPRO service
  • To send you account-related emails (receipts, updates, alerts)
  • To improve the product based on usage patterns
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data or your customers' data for advertising, marketing to third parties, or any purpose outside operating ReviewBoostPRO.

4. Your Customers' Data

When you add customer names and phone numbers to ReviewBoostPRO, you are the data controller for that information. We process it only on your behalf to send review requests as instructed by you.

We do not contact your customers independently, sell their data, or use it for any purpose other than sending the review requests you initiate.

You are responsible for ensuring you have appropriate consent from your customers to contact them via WhatsApp.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL database with row-level security). All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to your data is restricted to your account only.

We use industry-standard security practices and regularly review our systems for vulnerabilities.

6. Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

  • Supabase — our database provider (data storage)
  • Razorpay — payment processing
  • Google Analytics — anonymised website analytics

We never sell your data. We never share it with advertisers or marketing companies.

7. Cookies

We use minimal cookies necessary to keep you logged in and to collect anonymised analytics via Google Analytics (GA4). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@reviewboost.in

9. Data Deletion

When you delete your account, we delete all your personal data and customer data within 30 days. Some data may be retained longer where required by law (e.g. payment records for tax purposes).

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email. Continued use of ReviewBoostPRO after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at:
Email: support@reviewboost.in
Website: reviewboost.in
