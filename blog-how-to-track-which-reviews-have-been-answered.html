How to Track Which Reviews Have Been Answered (And Which Haven't)

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• September 14, 2026 • 7 min read

Imagine this scenario: You run a busy dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai, or a popular multi-cuisine restaurant in Koramangala, Bangalore. Every day, customers walk through your doors, avail your services, and occasionally leave reviews on your Google Business Profile. Some reviews are glowing five-star praises praising your polite staff or delicious food. Others are critical notes about a long waiting time or a billing error.

You reply to a few reviews on your smartphone while sipping morning chai, feeling like you have everything under control. But fast forward two weeks. A customer who left a critical two-star review about a delayed prescription at your Hyderabad pharmacy notices you never responded. Disappointed by the silence, they permanently take their business to the competitor down the street, and perhaps even downgrade their review to a harsh one-star.

Tracking which Google reviews have been answered and which are falling through the cracks is one of the most overlooked operational challenges for local businesses across India. In this comprehensive guide, we'll explore why tracking your replies matters immensely, the common pitfalls of manual tracking, and how modern automation tools can streamline your entire reputation management workflow.

Why Answering Google Reviews is Non-Negotiable

For Indian consumers today, Google Maps is the ultimate directory. Whether someone is searching for an "auto repair shop near me" in Pune or a "dermatologist in Chennai", they scroll straight through the ratings and reviews before making a choice.

When you respond to reviews, you are sending powerful signals to two distinct audiences:

  • Potential Customers: Prospective buyers read responses to gauge your customer service attitude. A polite, solution-oriented reply to a negative review often wins more trust than a page full of unblemished five-star ratings.
  • Google's Local Algorithm: Google's search algorithms favor active business profiles. Regularly responding to reviews signals that your business is open, engaged, and actively managed, which directly boosts your local map rankings.
The Cost of Silence According to consumer research, over 50% of customers expect local businesses to respond to negative reviews within a week. Ignoring them entirely often results in permanent customer churn and tarnished local credibility.

The Hidden Chaos of Manual Review Tracking

Most small and medium business owners in India start out managing their Google reviews manually. You log into the Google Business Profile dashboard on your laptop, or you rely on random notification banners popping up on your mobile phone.

However, as your footfall increases—say you are running a busy retail pharmacy in Delhi or a boutique salon in Ahmedabad—manual tracking quickly breaks down due to several structural issues:

  • Notification Fatigue: Business owners receive dozens of notifications daily for WhatsApp messages, emails, UPI payment alerts, and staff updates. Google review notifications easily get buried and lost.
  • Multi-Location Blindspots: If you manage two or three branches across different cities, logging into separate Google profiles to check for unanswered feedback becomes a daily administrative nightmare.
  • Lack of Accountability: When multiple staff members share access to a single account, nobody knows for sure whether the duty manager or receptionist already replied to a specific customer query.

Practical Methods to Track Answered vs. Unanswered Reviews

If you want to get a firm grip on your review pipeline without letting anything slip through the cracks, you need a systematic tracking approach. Here are the most effective ways local businesses in India maintain clarity:

1. Utilizing Google's Native Filters

Inside your Google Business Profile dashboard, navigate to the "Reviews" tab. Google allows you to filter reviews by those you have "Replied to" and those you "Haven't replied to". Make it a mandatory weekly ritual to check the "Haven't replied to" filter and clear out the queue.

2. Maintaining an Internal Spreadsheet Log

Some meticulous operations managers maintain a Google Sheet tracking every incoming review. Columns typically include Date, Customer Name, Rating, Branch Location, Review Text, Status (Answered/Pending), and Staff Member Responsible. While this works for smaller volumes, it requires tedious manual data entry.

Best Practices for Maintaining a Clean Review Queue

Tracking is only half the battle; maintaining a swift response turnaround time requires disciplined standard operating procedures (SOPs). Implement these straightforward rules in your business:

  • Establish Daily Audit Times: Dedicate 10 minutes every morning or evening specifically to review monitoring. Do not let pending reviews pile up over the weekend.
  • Use Template Variations: While personalization is key, having approved templates for thanking positive reviewers helps your team reply quickly and maintain brand voice consistency.
  • Prioritize Critical Feedback: Always sort your unanswered queue by lowest rating first. Addressing a one-star or two-star grievance takes precedence over acknowledging a five-star review.
