What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Says Nobody Responded to Them?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 14, 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this scenario: You are running a busy dental clinic in Mumbai or managing a popular multi-cuisine restaurant in Bangalore. Your front desk or reception staff is juggling walk-in inquiries, phone calls, WhatsApp messages, and billing. Amidst this chaos, a prospective client or frustrated customer drops a public Google review or leaves a message stating: "Tried calling and messaging multiple times, but absolutely nobody responded!"

For Indian business owners, hearing that a customer felt ignored is painful. In our hyper-connected digital landscape, response time is quite literally currency. When a customer feels neglected, they don't just walk away quietly; they often vent online, damaging the local reputation you have spent years building.

So, what should you actually reply when faced with this accusation? Let's break down the exact strategy, psychology, and ready-to-use templates to turn an angry grievance into a testimony of exceptional customer service.

Why "Nobody Responded" Complaints Hurt Local Businesses

In competitive Indian markets across Delhi, Chennai, Pune, and Hyderabad, consumers have endless options. If a patient cannot book an appointment at your pharmacy or clinic within minutes, they will simply click on the next listing on Google Maps. When they leave a review complaining about radio silence, prospective customers scrolling through your profile instantly assume your business is disorganized or unprofessional.

Worse yet, ignoring these complaints tells Google's algorithm that your customer engagement is poor. Responding promptly and professionally is critical not just for saving that specific relationship, but for showing hundreds of future readers that your management genuinely cares.

The Danger of Defensiveness Never reply with excuses like "Our staff was busy" or "You called outside working hours." Even if true, it shifts the blame onto the customer, making them feel invalidated and escalating their anger.

The Golden Rules of Replying to Unresponsive Complaints

Before you type out a response, keep these three foundational rules in mind:

  • Acknowledge immediately: Speed is everything. A reply within 2 hours neutralizes the anger.
  • Take ownership without making excuses: Validate their frustration right away.
  • Take the conversation offline: Provide a direct phone number or WhatsApp link to resolve the specific issue privately.

Ready-to-Use Response Templates for Indian Businesses

Depending on whether the complaint is on a public Google review platform or via a private messaging channel, here are proven scripts you can adapt:

Template 1: For a Public Google Review

"Dear [Customer Name], we are truly sorry to hear about your experience. This is certainly not the standard of service we strive to provide at our [clinic/salon/restaurant] in [City]. We would love to make this right immediately. Could you please call or WhatsApp our manager directly at [Phone Number]? We want to ensure your concern is addressed right away."

Template 2: For a WhatsApp or Direct Message Inquiry

"Namaste [Customer Name], apologies for the delayed response! Our team was attending to heavy patient/customer flow. Thank you for pointing this out. How can we assist you right now? Let's get your requirement sorted immediately."
