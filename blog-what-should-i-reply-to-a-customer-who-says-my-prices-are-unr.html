What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Says My Prices Are Unreasonable?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this: You wake up, open your Google Business Profile dashboard, and see a fresh 1-star review from someone who visited your establishment yesterday. You open it up, and your heart sinks. The review reads: "Extremely overpriced! Charging ₹1,500 for a service that takes twenty minutes is completely unreasonable. Will never visit again."

As a local business owner in India—whether you run a dental clinic in South Mumbai, a trendy salon in Bandra, an auto repair garage in Pune, or a boutique hotel in Jaipur—a price complaint stings differently than a standard service issue. It attacks the very core of your business model, your overhead, your expertise, and your livelihood.

Your immediate instinct might be to fire back defensively. You want to type out a furious paragraph explaining the rising cost of raw materials, the 18% GST, rent inflation in prime commercial hubs like Bangalore or Delhi, and the years of professional training you invested in. Take a deep breath. Replying defensively to a price complaint on a public forum is one of the fastest ways to turn a single disgruntled consumer into a permanent PR nightmare for your brand.

How you handle an online review about pricing doesn't just impact that single individual. Prospective customers across India read these replies to judge your professionalism, your empathy, and the actual value you deliver. Done right, a price complaint can actually become an incredible conversion tool that highlights your superior quality and justifies every single rupee you charge.

Why Price Complaints Happen on Google Reviews

Before crafting a response, it helps to understand where the reviewer is coming from. In the hyper-competitive Indian market, consumers are more price-conscious than ever. Deal-hunting apps, flash sales, and discount culture have trained people to look for the absolute lowest denominator.

When a customer leaves a negative review about your prices, it typically stems from one of three scenarios:

  • Mismatched Expectations: The buyer did not understand what was included in the price package before making the purchase or booking the appointment.
  • Value Perception Gap: They paid your price, but because of poor communication or lack of perceived quality, they felt the outcome did not match the money spent.
  • The Bargain Hunter Mentality: Some individuals simply look for cheap rates rather than quality outcomes, and when they encounter professional-grade pricing, they take it personally.
The Golden Rule of Public Replies Never argue about money in a public forum. Your goal when replying to a pricing review is never to win an argument with the reviewer, but to demonstrate grace, transparency, and confidence to the hundreds of future customers reading your response.

The 3-Step Formula for Responding to Price Reviews

An effective response to an expensive-rate complaint follows a clear, structured psychological path. You must acknowledge, clarify (without sounding defensive), and offline the conversation.

Step 1: Acknowledge and Validate (Politely)
Start by thanking them for taking the time to share feedback. Acknowledge that you understand cost is an important factor for every customer. Never invalidate their feelings; instead, shift the focus toward value.

Step 2: Highlight Value and Transparency
Gently remind the reader what your price actually includes. Mention genuine cost drivers without sounding like you are whining. For instance, mention certified products, expert technicians, hygiene standards, or premium parts.

Step 3: Move the Discussion Offline
Never negotiate refunds or argue specifics in public comments. Provide a direct phone number or email address where they can speak directly with the manager or owner.

Ready-to-Use Templates for Indian Local Businesses

Depending on your industry, your response style might vary. Here are field-tested templates tailored for different types of local establishments across India:

We would love to discuss your experience further—please reach out to our clinic manager directly at [Phone Number]."

We take great pride in providing premium styling, 100% authentic international hair care products, and highly trained stylists who undergo continuous advanced training. Template 3: For Auto Repair and Service Centers
"Hello [Name], we appreciate you bringing this to our attention. At our workshop in Hyderabad, our service charges include genuine OEM spare parts, advanced diagnostic tools, and comprehensive warranties on all repair work to ensure your vehicle's safety on the road. We are transparent about our estimates before starting any job. Please call our service head at [Phone Number] to discuss this so we can clarify any billing doubts you may have."
