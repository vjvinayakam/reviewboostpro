What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Complains About Hidden Charges?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial
• August 14, 2026 • 7 min read

Nothing strikes fear into the heart of a local business owner quite like a one-star Google review shouting about "hidden charges." Whether you run a diagnostic clinic in South Mumbai, a popular family restaurant in Bangalore, a premium salon in Hyderabad, or an auto repair workshop in Pune, pricing transparency is the bedrock of consumer trust in India today.

When a customer feels short-changed—perhaps due to an unmentioned GST levy, extra service fees, delivery charges, or material costs added to the final bill—they take straight to Google. Left unaddressed or handled poorly, that review brands your business as dishonest to thousands of potential local customers searching maps on their smartphones.

So, what is the exact protocol? How do you reply professionally, protect your brand image, and potentially turn a furious customer into an advocate? Let's break down the psychology of price complaints and give you actionable templates tailored for Indian businesses.

Why "Hidden Charge" Complaints Are So Dangerous

In the Indian retail and service landscape, value consciousness is paramount. Customers budget meticulously. When a bill arrives with an unexpected ₹500 or ₹1,000 addition—even if completely justified by taxes, packaging, or specialized labour—it feels like a betrayal of trust.

When this grievance is posted publicly on Google Business Profile, it doesn't just attack your revenue; it attacks your integrity. Prospective clients reading the review won't analyze the operational nuance of your pricing model. They will simply think: "This business tries to trick people." This makes immediate, calibrated damage control mandatory.

The Golden Rule of Public Replies Never argue, accuse the customer of lying, or get defensive in public view. Your reply is not meant to win an argument with the angry reviewer—it is meant to show every future reader that your business is mature, empathetic, and professional.

Step 1: Acknowledge and De-escalate Immediately

Your first sentence must lower the temperature. Acknowledge that they are frustrated without necessarily admitting fault if the charge was legitimate and communicated beforehand. Empathy goes a long way in Indian customer service.

Instead of saying "Our menu clearly states taxes are extra," try:

  • "Hi [Name], thank you for sharing your feedback. I completely understand how an unexpected addition to your final bill can be frustrating, and I apologize for any miscommunication."
  • "Hello [Name], we always strive for absolute transparency with our pricing at our [clinic/restaurant/salon] in Delhi, so I am concerned to hear you felt surprised by the billing."

Step 2: Take the Conversation Offline Instantly

Public back-and-forth about numbers, receipts, and policies looks messy and rarely ends well. Move the discussion to a private channel as quickly as possible. Provide a direct phone number or name someone who can personally resolve the matter.

For instance: "We want to look into your exact bill and clarify how this occurred. Could you please reach out to our manager, Rajesh, directly at [Phone Number] or drop us a message? We want to make this right for you."

Ready-to-Use Reply Templates for Indian Businesses

Depending on whether the charge was a genuine misunderstanding, a system error, or a standard tax/service fee, use these field-tested templates:

Template A: For standard taxes/service charges that were missed during booking
"Hi [Customer Name], thank you for your feedback. We apologize if the inclusion of [GST / packaging charges / service fees] wasn't clearly communicated to you upfront during your visit to our Mumbai location. Transparency is extremely important to us. Please call our manager at [Phone Number] so we can review your invoice together and ensure you are completely satisfied."

Template B: When there was an actual billing mistake or miscalculation
"Hello [Customer Name], we are deeply sorry about this error in your billing. This is certainly not the standard of service we aim for at our Bangalore store. We would love the opportunity to immediately rectify this and process a refund for the extra amount charged. Please connect with our team at [Phone Number] at your earliest convenience."

Step 3: Fix the Root Cause in Your Operations

Replying to the review is only half the battle. If multiple customers are complaining about hidden charges, your pricing communication is genuinely broken. Take corrective operational steps:

  • Update Menus and Rate Cards: Ensure all taxes (GST), packaging charges, or consultation fees are explicitly printed as "inclusive of all taxes" or clearly marked as excluded before orders/appointments are confirmed.
  • Train Your Frontline Staff: Receptionists, billing counters, and waiters in Chennai, Kolkata, or Ahmedabad must verbally remind customers about extra charges before rendering the service or serving the meal.
  • Digital Confirmation: Send instant itemized WhatsApp estimates before starting major auto repairs or medical procedures.
