How to Respond to a Fake Google Review: A Complete Guide for Indian Business Owners

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 14, 2026 • 8 min read

Picture this: It is a busy Saturday morning at your multi-speciality dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai, or perhaps your family restaurant in Koramangala, Bangalore. You open your Google Business Profile, expecting to see glowing feedback from yesterday's happy customers. Instead, you are greeted by a scathing 1-star review from an anonymous user claiming terrible service, rude staff, and complete unprofessionalism.

You rack your brains. You check your appointment registries and customer logs. No one matching that name ever visited your premises. Panic sets in. You realize you have just been hit by a fake Google review—a malicious attack orchestrated either by an unscrupulous competitor, a disgruntled former employee, or simply an internet troll.

For local businesses across India, online reputation is everything. A single unaddressed 1-star review can deter dozens of potential clients from walking through your doors, translating to thousands of rupees in lost revenue. However, reacting out of anger or ignoring the review entirely are both catastrophic mistakes. In this comprehensive guide, we will walk you through how to spot, report, and professionally respond to fake Google reviews to protect your local business brand.

Step 1: How to Identify a Fake Google Review

Before you draft a response or report the review to Google, you must be 100% certain it is fake. Genuine negative reviews often contain specific details about a real interaction—a particular dish that was cold, a specific billing error, or a named staff member. Fake reviews, on the other hand, typically share a few distinct red flags:

  • Vague, emotional language: Words like "worst service ever," "terrible experience," or "absolute scam" without any specifics regarding what actually happened.
  • No matching records: When you search your CRM, POS system, or patient management software for the reviewer's name or contact info, you find zero history of them visiting your establishment.
  • Competitor footprints: Sometimes, the reviewer’s public profile shows they only review businesses in your exact niche within the same city—often leaving 1-star reviews for you and 5-star reviews for a rival business down the street.
  • Sudden review bursts: Receiving multiple 1-star ratings within the span of an hour from newly created Google accounts with stock photos.
Important Reality Check Never accuse the reviewer of being a liar or fake in your public response. Even if you know for a fact they never stepped foot in your Delhi pharmacy or Pune auto repair shop, prospective customers reading the thread later will judge your professionalism by how calm and factual you remain.

Step 2: How to Report and Flag the Review to Google

Google has clear content guidelines regarding spam and fake engagement. If a review violates their terms of service, you can request its removal. Here is how you do it:

  1. Log in to your Google Business Profile account via desktop or the Google Maps app.
  2. Navigate to your business listing and click on Reviews.
  3. Locate the suspicious fake review, click the three vertical dots next to it, and select Flag as inappropriate.
  4. Fill out the reporting form and select the violation category (e.g., Spam, Conflict of Interest, or Harassment).

Keep in mind that Google's automated moderation can take anywhere from a few days to a couple of weeks to process flagging requests. If the review is not removed automatically after flagging, you can escalate the ticket via the Google Business Profile Help Community where product experts can review blatant violations.

Step 3: Crafting the Perfect Professional Response

While you wait for Google to review your flagging request, you must write a public response. Remember: This response is not for the troll who wrote the review; it is for the hundreds of prospective local customers who will read it next week.

Your goal is to demonstrate that your business takes feedback seriously, while subtly signaling to human readers that this review does not add up.

Here is a proven template you can adapt for your Indian business:

"Hello [Name or 'Valued Customer'], we take our service standards extremely seriously at [Business Name]. However, after thoroughly checking our customer logs and appointment records for [City], we are unable to find any record of a transaction or visit under your name. We want to ensure every customer has a genuine and wonderful experience with us. Could you please reach out directly to our manager at [Phone Number/Email] with details of your visit so we can investigate this immediately?" — Standard Professional Response Framework

By writing a response like this, you achieve three vital objectives: First, you remain courteous and professional. Second, you subtly alert prospective customers that this reviewer's identity cannot be verified in your system. Third, you invite offline communication, which fake reviewers almost never pursue.

Summary Action Plan for Local Businesses

Dealing with fake reviews is an unfortunate reality of doing business online today, but it doesn't have to hurt your bottom line. Follow this checklist whenever you spot suspicious feedback:

  • Stay calm: Do not post an emotional or angry reply that can be used against you.
  • Investigate: Cross-reference your billing and appointment systems to verify if the person was ever a customer.
  • Flag it: Report the review immediately to Google for violating spam or conflict-of-interest guidelines.
  • Reply professionally: Write a polite message stating you have no record of their visit and invite them to connect offline.
  • Outnumber the fakes: Continuously gather real 5-star reviews via automated WhatsApp workflows to protect your stellar brand image.

By maintaining a disciplined, professional approach and keeping your genuine customer feedback pipeline full, you can render fake reviews completely powerless against your growing local enterprise.
