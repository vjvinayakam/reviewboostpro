How to Send a Google Review Link to Customers on WhatsApp

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 2026 • 8 min read

If you run a local business in India—whether it's a bustling dental clinic in South Mumbai, a popular South Indian restaurant in Bangalore, a trendy hair salon in Bandra, or an auto repair workshop in Pune—you already know how important Google Maps is. When potential customers search for services nearby, Google's Local Pack can make or break your month. And what drives those rankings faster than anything else? Authentic 5-star Google reviews.

However, getting customers to actually sit down and write a review has always been an uphill battle. Handing out printed QR code cards at the billing counter often results in lost paper or forgotten intentions. Sending generic SMS messages yields abysmal click-through rates because nobody reads SMS anymore. That leaves us with the undisputed king of communication in India: WhatsApp.

In this comprehensive guide, we will walk you through everything you need to know about how to send a Google review link to your customers on WhatsApp effectively, how to craft messages that convert, and how to automate the entire process so your staff doesn't have to lift a finger.

Why WhatsApp is the Ultimate Channel for Google Reviews in India

Let's look at the numbers. India is one of WhatsApp's largest global markets, with over 500 million active users. From teenagers in Delhi to grandmothers in Chennai managing family WhatsApp groups, checking WhatsApp is the very first thing most Indians do in the morning.

Unlike email—which sits unread in a promotional folder—or SMS—which is cluttered with spam from banks and telemarketers—WhatsApp messages have an open rate of over 98%. When a customer walks out of your clinic or receives their bill at your restaurant, sending a friendly, personalized WhatsApp message hits them right in their pocket while your service is still fresh in their minds.

Pro Tip for Local Businesses Timing is everything. Send your Google review request message within 30 to 60 minutes of the customer completing their transaction or visit. If you wait until the next day, their excitement fades, and your conversion rates will drop by more than half.

Step 1: How to Find Your Direct Google Review Link

Before you can send a link on WhatsApp, you need to make sure you have the cleanest, shortest link possible. Long, messy URLs look suspicious and lower click rates. Here is how to get your short review link:

  • Log in to your Google Business Profile (GBP) manager dashboard using your business Google account.
  • Navigate to your business profile home page.
  • Click on the "Get more reviews" button or card.
  • A popup window will display a short link (usually in the format of https://g.page/r/... or a shortened search link).
  • Copy this link and save it securely. If the link is too long, you can use a trusted URL shortener, but ensure your domain looks reputable.

Step 2: Crafting High-Converting WhatsApp Templates

Nobody likes getting robotic, spammy messages. To get customers to tap your link and leave glowing 5-star reviews, your message needs to feel warm, polite, and respectful of their time. Here are a few high-converting templates tailored for different Indian businesses:

For Clinics & Healthcare Providers:
"Hello [Customer Name], thank you for visiting Dr. Sharma's Clinic today in Delhi. We hope you are feeling better! Could you take 30 seconds to share your experience on Google? It helps other patients find reliable care: [Insert Link]. Wishing you good health!"

For Restaurants & Cafes:
"Hi [Customer Name], thank you for dining with us at Spice Garden in Bangalore tonight! We loved hosting you. If you enjoyed the food, we would be thrilled if you could drop us a quick 5-star review here: [Insert Link]. See you again soon!"

For Salons & Spas:
"Hey [Customer Name]! We hope you love your new look from Glamour Studio in Mumbai. ✨ If you enjoyed our service today, a quick review on Google would mean the world to our team: [Insert Link]. Have a wonderful week!"

Best Practices for WhatsApp Review Requests

To maximize your success rate when sending review links over WhatsApp, keep these golden rules in mind:

  • Always personalize: Use the customer's first name. Automated messages that feel human get significantly higher response rates.
  • Keep it brief: Indian consumers scan WhatsApp messages in seconds. Do not write paragraphs. Get straight to the point and provide the link clearly.
  • Never buy fake reviews: Buying fake reviews from shady agencies can get your Google Business Profile suspended overnight. Stick to authentic, organic reviews from real local customers via WhatsApp.
  • Express gratitude: Always thank the customer, whether they leave a review immediately or not. Building long-term goodwill matters more than a single transaction.
