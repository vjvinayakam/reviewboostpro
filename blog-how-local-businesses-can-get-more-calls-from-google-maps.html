How Local Businesses Can Get More Calls From Google Maps

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 2026 • 9 min read

Picture this: It is a busy Saturday evening in Mumbai or Bangalore. A potential customer is standing in traffic, frantically searching their phone for "best dental clinic near me" or "emergency AC repair around me." They open Google Maps, see three prominent listings at the top of the local map pack, and tap the call button on one of them. Within thirty seconds, that business has secured a high-intent lead.

Now, ask yourself: Is your local business—whether it is a pharmacy in Delhi, a hair salon in Pune, or an auto repair shop in Chennai—the one getting that phone call? Or are you buried on page two while your competitors capture all the revenue?

For Indian local businesses, Google Maps has completely replaced traditional Yellow Pages and printed directories. When people need immediate services, they turn to their smartphones. However, simply creating a Google Business Profile (GBP) and forgetting about it is no longer enough. To truly dominate local search results and drive a steady stream of phone inquiries, you need a deliberate, data-driven local SEO strategy.

The Hidden Cost of Inaction Over 70% of local smartphone searches result in a direct action—such as a phone call or a store visit. If your Google Maps listing lacks optimized keywords, fresh reviews, and accurate data, you are handing paying customers directly to your neighborhood competitors every single day.

1. Optimize Your Google Business Profile for Intent-Driven Keywords

The foundation of getting phone calls from Google Maps starts with your Google Business Profile optimization. Google's algorithm does not just look at your business name; it reads every single word in your profile to understand what you offer and where you operate.

Many Indian business owners make the mistake of keeping their business title minimal—for example, just writing "Sharma Clinic" or "Gupta Motors." Instead, strategically incorporate your primary service and location into your secondary fields, or ensure your official store signage matches your profile name cleanly. For instance, "Sharma Dental Clinic & Orthodontic Center - Andheri West" instantly tells both Google and prospective patients what services you provide.

Furthermore, select your primary and secondary categories with absolute precision. If you own an auto repair shop, do not just select "Car Repair." Add secondary categories like "Car Air Conditioning Service," "Auto Transmission Shop," and "Wheel Alignment Service." Each category opens up new keyword matching opportunities on Google Maps, increasing your overall visibility.

2. Supercharge Your Local Rankings with Fresh Reviews

Visibility on Google Maps is great, but visibility without trust equals zero phone calls. When a customer in Hyderabad searches for a trusted dermatologist, they look at two things instantly: your star rating and the number of reviews you possess.

Google heavily favors businesses that consistently generate new, authentic reviews with relevant keywords. A clinic with 350 five-star reviews will almost always outrank a competitor with 12 reviews, even if the competitor is slightly closer geographically. Furthermore, customers naturally gravitate towards businesses that look active, popular, and deeply trusted by their local community.

Gathering these reviews used to be an administrative nightmare. Asking customers verbally as they exit your store or clinic often resulted in them forgetting by the time they reached home. This is where modern automation transforms operations. Because WhatsApp boasts nearly 98% open rates in India, customers tap the link and leave a Google review in under 30 seconds while the positive experience is still fresh in their minds.

Pro Tip for Higher Conversion When customers write reviews mentioning specific services (e.g., "Best root canal treatment in Chennai"), Google indexes those keywords. This creates a powerful SEO feedback loop that drives even higher rankings for those specific services on Maps.

3. Ensure NAP Consistency Across the Web

NAP stands for Name, Address, and Phone Number. Google's local search algorithm cross-references your Google Business Profile data with other mentions of your business across the internet—such as Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, your Facebook page, and your official website.

If your address is listed as "Shop 4, MG Road, Bangalore" on Google Maps, but appears as "No. 4/B, Mahatma Gandhi Road, Bangalore" on Justdial, Google's algorithm gets confused. Confusion leads to lower trust scores, which ultimately suppresses your ranking in the local map pack.

Conduct a thorough audit of your digital footprint. Ensure your business phone number is formatted consistently and that you use a local Indian telephone code or active mobile number that is always answered during business hours. Missing a call from an urgent Google Maps lead means losing that customer forever.

4. Leverage Google Posts and Q&A to Answer Objections

Most business owners treat their Google Business Profile like a static digital visiting card. Successful local businesses treat it like an interactive landing page.

Utilize the Google Posts feature weekly to publish updates, special discounts, festive offers (like Diwali or Pongal promotions), and new service launches. These updates signal to Google that your business is actively managed and operational.

Additionally, monitor the Q&A section of your profile religiously. Prospective customers often ask questions directly on your listing, such as: "Do you offer home sample collection for blood tests?" or "Is parking available?" If you leave these questions unanswered, potential callers may hesitate and call a competitor instead. Answer every question promptly, and proactively seed your Q&A section with FAQs containing your primary keywords.

5. Track Your Performance and Optimize Continuously

You cannot improve what you do not measure. Google Business Profile provides robust built-in insights that show you exactly how many people viewed your listing, whether they found you through direct searches (typing your brand name) or discovery searches (typing general terms like "best restaurant near me"), and crucially—how many users clicked the "Call" button.

Set aside 15 minutes every fortnight to review your analytics:

  • Are your phone call metrics trending upward?
  • Which search queries are driving the most map impressions?
  • Are you responding to all new customer reviews within 24 hours? (Responding to reviews with thoughtful replies also signals high engagement to Google's ranking algorithm.)
