What to Do When the Same Customer Keeps Leaving Negative Reviews

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 14, 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this: You run a busy dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai, or a popular North Indian restaurant in Connaught Place, New Delhi. You log into your Google Business Profile to check your local reputation. You see a fresh 1-star rating. You open it up, and your heart sinks. It is not just a disgruntled patron—it is that customer again. The one who left a scathing review last month, edited it last week, and is now dropping yet another low rating under a slightly different display name or via a secondary Google account.

Dealing with a single negative review is standard operating procedure for any Indian business owner. But when the same individual wages an ongoing campaign of harassment, targeting your livelihood with repetitive bad ratings, it crosses the line from customer feedback into targeted sabotage. It drains your mental energy, harms your local SEO rankings, and deters potential walk-ins across Chennai, Bangalore, and Pune who judge your business at a single glance.

How do you stop a serial negative reviewer without violating Google’s content policies or escalating an already volatile situation? Let’s break down a tactical, step-by-step game plan.

1. Diagnose the Root Cause: Real Grievance vs. Malicious Trolling

Before taking any action, take a deep breath and analyze the situation objectively. Ask yourself: Is this an unresolved service issue, an unrealistic customer expectation, or an act of sheer malice?

Often, repeat reviewers feel ignored. Perhaps Ramesh in Pune visited your auto repair shop, felt overcharged for brake pad replacements, and believes shouting online is his only recourse. Alternatively, it could be a disgruntled ex-employee, a direct competitor operating in the same lane, or a pure troll.

Crucial Distinction If the reviewer has a legitimate, unresolved grievance, no amount of technical reporting will permanently silence them until their core issue is addressed offline. If it is pure harassment or spam, your playbook shifts entirely to documentation and policy enforcement.

2. Craft a Professional Public Response (For Future Customers)

Never forget that your response to a repeat negative reviewer is not actually meant for them—it is meant for the hundreds of prospective customers in Hyderabad or Ahmedabad who will read this thread tomorrow.

When the same person posts a second or third negative review, do not respond with anger, sarcasm, or defensive accusations. Keep your tone remarkably calm, polite, and detached. Demonstrate to the public that your business is professional while quietly establishing a paper trail of unresponsiveness from their end.

  • Acknowledge and pivot: "Hello [Name], we note that you have left another review regarding your visit last month..."
  • State the facts gently: "...as we mentioned in our previous response and via our manager's direct calls, we have invited you multiple times to discuss a refund or resolution."
  • Take it offline immediately: "Please reach out to our grievance officer directly at support@yourbusiness.in so we can finally resolve this matter."

By doing this, any neutral reader scanning your Google listing instantly realizes that you are reasonable and that the reviewer is behaving unreasonably.

3. Flag and Report Violations to Google Support

Google’s Terms of Service explicitly prohibit harassment, spam, and conflicts of interest. When the same customer bombards your listing with multiple reviews, duplicate accounts, or abusive language, they are violating Google’s Contributor Policy.

Here is how you escalate the issue effectively:

  • Go to your Google Business Profile dashboard and locate the offending review.
  • Click the three dots next to the review and select "Report review".
  • Choose the most accurate violation category (e.g., "Spam", "Harassment", or "Conflict of Interest").
  • If the review is not removed automatically within 48 hours, visit the Google Business Profile Help Community. Create a detailed post outlining the harassment, providing screenshots that prove the user is repeatedly spamming your listing. Google product experts frequently review these forum threads and escalate persistent malicious cases.

4. Drown Out the Noise with a Flood of Genuine Positive Reviews

While you fight bureaucratic review removal processes, your local search visibility remains vulnerable. A couple of stubborn 1-star ratings can tank your overall rating from 4.6 to 4.1, heavily impacting your local pack rankings.

The best long-term defense against repeat trolls is volume. When you have hundreds of glowing 5-star reviews from genuine, happy Indian customers, a stray repetitive bad review becomes statistical white noise that no prospective customer takes seriously.

5. When to Involve Legal Counsel

If the harassment crosses the threshold into defamation, financial sabotage, or targeted stalking—which sometimes happens with unscrupulous competitors or deeply unstable individuals—you have legal recourse under Indian law.

Consult a Cyber Cell advocate or civil litigation lawyer. Under Section 499/500 of the Indian Penal Code (and corresponding sections of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita), criminal defamation suits can be filed against individuals posting false, malicious statements intended to harm business reputation. Often, a formal legal notice sent to the individual's registered address or phone number is enough to make them delete their accounts and vanish permanently.

Summary Checklist for Business Owners

When lightning strikes twice from the same negative reviewer, follow this systematic checklist:

  • Stay emotionless: Never reply in anger or get into a public argument.
  • Respond for the audience: Write your reply to reassure future prospective buyers looking at your profile.
  • Report aggressively: Flag duplicate reviews and escalate to the Google Help Community.
  • Drown them out: Collect a steady stream of authentic reviews using automated tools like ReviewBoostPRO so negative outliers hold zero weight.
  • Consult legal support: Escalate to formal legal notices if harassment turns into systematic defamation.

Remember, a negative review campaign is frustrating, but it is also temporary. By keeping your cool, tightening your documentation, and overwhelming your profile with genuine customer love, you will protect your brand's reputation and continue growing your local business across India.
