What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Says They Were Overcharged?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Nothing stings a local business owner quite like seeing a 1-star Google review accusing you of looting or overcharging. Whether you run a bustling dental clinic in South Mumbai, a popular family restaurant in Bangalore's Indiranagar, or a car repair workshop in Pune, price complaints hit close to home. In the Indian market, where consumers are fiercely value-conscious and quick to compare every rupee, a public allegation of high pricing can severely damage your local reputation if left unchecked.

When a customer writes, "They charged me ₹1,500 for something that costs ₹500 elsewhere!" or "Hidden fees added at the billing counter!" your immediate reaction might be defensive anger. You want to type out an angry rebuttal detailing every single line item, tax component, and overhead cost. But take a deep breath. How you reply to this review isn't just about answering that specific customer—it is about signaling your credibility to hundreds of potential future customers who are reading your response.

Why Price Complaint Reviews Require Immediate Tact

In India, trust is built on transparency. When a prospective client looks at your Google Business Profile, they don't just look at your average star rating; they read how you handle conflict. If they see an aggressive business owner fighting with customers over money, they will walk away. Conversely, if they see a calm, empathetic, and professional explanation, they will respect your business all the more.

Price complaints generally stem from three distinct scenarios:

  • Miscommunication: The customer did not fully understand what was included in the service or product package (e.g., GST, extra labor charges, or premium ingredients).
  • Billing Errors: A genuine human mistake occurred at the cash counter or billing desk.
  • Expectation Mismatch: The customer expected local street-side pricing while visiting a professional establishment with standardized overheads and quality guarantees.
The Golden Rule of Public Replies Never argue numbers in public. State your commitment to fairness briefly, then immediately shift the conversation to a private channel (like a phone call or WhatsApp) to resolve the actual financial dispute.

Step-by-Step Framework for Responding to Overcharging Claims

Crafting the perfect reply requires a careful balance of empathy, professionalism, and firmness without sounding arrogant. Here is a proven framework you can adapt for your clinic, salon, or retail outlet:

1. Acknowledge and Remain Polite: Start by addressing the reviewer by name (if available) and thanking them for sharing their feedback. This shows you take customer experience seriously.

2. Clarify Value Without Being Defensive: Gently remind them of the quality, genuine parts, expert consultation, or taxes included in the bill. Never say, "You are wrong and cheap." Instead, say, "Our pricing reflects genuine certified components and standard service taxes."

3. Take It Offline Instantly: Provide a direct phone number or manager's contact so the customer can share their bill invoice for verification.

How to Prevent Price Complaints Before They Happen Online

The best way to handle a negative review about overcharging is to prevent it from ever being posted on Google in the first place. Most customers only vent online because they felt ignored or brushed off at the billing counter.

Train your billing staff in Mumbai, Delhi, or Hyderabad to always explain the bill clearly before the customer pays. If a customer hesitates or questions a price, address it right then and there with patience. Offer a clear breakdown of taxes (GST), service charges, or premium additions.

Furthermore, maintaining a steady stream of positive feedback acts as your ultimate shield. When satisfied customers leave glowing reviews daily, an isolated price complaint loses its sting. Future customers reading that reply will respect your integrity immensely, often turning a negative situation into a powerful trust-builder.
