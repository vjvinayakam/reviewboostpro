How to Report a Fake Google Review: Step-by-Step Guide for Indian Businesses

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this: You open your Google Business Profile dashboard on a busy Tuesday morning at your dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai. You expect to see notes from patients thanking your staff, but instead, your rating has dropped overnight. A 1-star review sits proudly at the top, written by someone who has never stepped foot in your clinic, complaining about a completely fabricated treatment gone wrong.

If you run a local business in India—whether it is a bustling restaurant in Indiranagar (Bangalore), a busy pharmacy in Connaught Place (Delhi), a car repair workshop in Pune, or a salon in Chennai—dealing with malicious, fake, or spam reviews is a frustrating reality. Online reputation directly impacts footfall and revenue. A sudden surge of negative, unverified feedback can cost you thousands of rupees in lost customers.

Fortunately, Google provides mechanisms to flag and remove reviews that violate its content guidelines. In this comprehensive guide, we will walk you through exactly how to spot fake reviews, report them effectively, and safeguard your local business's hard-earned digital reputation.

1. How to Identify a Genuine Negative Review vs. a Fake One

Before you hit the report button, you need to be certain the review violates Google’s terms of service. Google will not remove a review simply because you disagree with it or because a customer had a genuine bad experience. It must violate Google Maps User Generated Content Policy.

Look out for these common warning signs of a fake review:

  • No matching records in your database: Check your patient, customer, or client logs. If the name provided matches no one who has visited your Mumbai clinic or Hyderabad restaurant, it is a massive red flag.
  • Vague descriptions: The reviewer uses overly general complaints like "Terrible service" or "Worst experience ever" without mentioning specific dates, names of staff members, or actual services rendered.
  • Competitor activity: The profile leaving the review frequently reviews competing local businesses in the same neighborhood within minutes of each other.
  • Sudden review bursts: Receiving 5, 10, or 20 one-star reviews within a span of a few hours usually indicates a coordinated bot attack or malicious spam.
Important Rule of Thumb Do not panic or reply emotionally in public. Replying with an aggressive paragraph can make your business look unprofessional to prospective customers browsing your profile in Delhi or Chennai. Stay calm, document everything, and proceed to reporting.

2. Step-by-Step: How to Report a Fake Review via Google Maps

Reporting a review is a straightforward process, but you must follow Google's official pathway to ensure your case is reviewed by human moderators or automated filters.

Follow these steps from your desktop or smartphone:

  • Open Google Maps or navigate directly to your Google Business Profile on your browser while logged into your management account.
  • Locate your business listing and click on your reviews section.
  • Scroll down to find the specific fake review you wish to challenge.
  • Click or tap on the three vertical dots (the menu icon) located next to the reviewer's name.
  • Select "Report review" from the drop-down menu.
  • A new window will open on Google Support asking you to categorize the violation. Choose the most accurate violation type (e.g., Spam, Conflict of interest, Off-topic, or Misinformation).
  • Submit your report.

Once submitted, Google’s automated system and moderation team will evaluate the review against their policy guidelines. This process typically takes anywhere from 3 to 7 business days.

3. Escalating Through the Google Business Profile Help Community

Sometimes, simply hitting "Report" on Google Maps does not yield results, especially if automated filters fail to catch sophisticated spam accounts. If your initial report is rejected, you have a second line of defense: The Google Business Profile Help Community Forum.

Here is how to escalate your case:

  • Go to the official Google Business Profile Help Community.
  • Click on "Ask now" to create a new post detailing your issue.
  • Provide clear screenshots of the fake review.
  • Write a polite, factual explanation stating why the review violates policies (e.g., providing proof that the reviewer is a known competitor or a bot account).
  • Product Experts (trusted community volunteers) monitor these forums and can escalate blatant violations directly to Google internal teams for manual review.
