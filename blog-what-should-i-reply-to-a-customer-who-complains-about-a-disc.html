What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Complains About a Discount?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 14, 2026 • 8 min read

Running a local business in bustling commercial hubs like Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, or Chennai comes with its unique set of daily challenges. Among them, dealing with price-sensitive consumers is a universal reality. In India, bargaining and looking for the best "deal" is woven deep into our shopping culture. From roadside vegetable vendors in Pune to high-end dental clinics in Hyderabad, every business owner eventually hears the inevitable phrase: "Arey, itna mehenga? Koi discount nahi milega kya?" (Isn't this too expensive? Aren't you giving any discount?)

However, the situation turns sticky when a customer complains publicly—either face-to-face in front of other patrons, or worse, by leaving a negative 1-star Google review complaining that your prices are too high or your promotional discount wasn't applied to their satisfaction. How you reply to these complaints can either make you look professional and firm or desperate and defensive.

In this guide, we will break down exactly how Indian business owners can craft the perfect response to discount complaints, protect their profit margins, maintain their local brand reputation, and transform critical remarks into loyal patronage.

1. Understand the Psychology Behind Indian Price Complaints

Before typing out a defensive reply, it is vital to understand why an Indian consumer complains about discounts or pricing. In most cases, it is rarely personal. It stems from a desire to extract maximum value for hard-earned rupees. Whether they are paying for a root canal at a dental clinic, a hair smoothing treatment at a luxury salon, or regular servicing at an auto repair shop, customers want to feel like they scored a win.

When a customer leaves a negative review complaining about a lack of discounts, they are usually expressing frustration over perceived unfairness—believing that someone else got a better deal or that your standard rates do not match the perceived quality. Recognizing this underlying emotion allows you to respond with empathy rather than anger.

⚠️ What NOT To Do Never reply with sarcasm, quote your raw operational costs line-by-line, or argue with the customer in public view. Saying things like "If you want cheap quality, go somewhere else" will permanently alienate hundreds of prospective local customers browsing your Google Business Profile.

2. Crafting the Public Google Review Response

When a discount complaint lands on your Google Business Profile, your primary audience isn't actually the complaining customer—it is the hundreds of potential local clients who will read that review tomorrow. Your response must demonstrate absolute professionalism, courtesy, and firmness regarding your pricing structure.

Here is a proven template you can adapt for your clinic, restaurant, or store:

  • Acknowledge and Validate: Thank them for visiting your establishment and sharing their perspective.
  • Highlight Value and Quality: Briefly remind readers of the premium materials, certified staff, or exceptional service standards that justify your pricing.
  • Direct to Private Channel: Invite them to speak with the manager or owner directly via phone or WhatsApp to resolve any misunderstanding.

Example Response for a Salon in Bangalore:
"Hi Rajesh, thank you for visiting our salon in Indiranagar. We always strive to provide premium, salon-grade products and expert styling to our clients. We do run seasonal promotional offers and loyalty discounts from time to time, and we regret if our pricing structure wasn't clear during your visit. Please drop us a WhatsApp message at our business number so we can look into your bill and see how we can make it right for your next visit!"

3. Shifting the Conversation to Private Channels (Like WhatsApp)

Public comment sections are terrible venues for negotiating discounts or resolving monetary disputes. Once the initial public reply is posted, your goal should be to move the conversation offline or onto a direct messaging platform. In India, WhatsApp is the undisputed king of business communication.

By shifting the disgruntled customer to WhatsApp, you humanize your business. You can send them a polite voice note, share custom loyalty coupons, or explain your pricing tiers in a conversational manner without inviting a public spectacle.

4. Proactive Strategies to Prevent Discount Complaints

The best way to handle a complaint about a discount is to eliminate the root cause before the customer ever reaches the billing counter or the review page. Transparency is your greatest shield against price friction.

Local businesses across Mumbai and Delhi have found immense success by implementing these proactive measures:

  • Clear Menu and Price Boards: Ensure all service charges, taxes, and add-ons are clearly displayed upfront so there are no billing shocks.
  • Transparent Offer Terms: If you run festival discounts (Diwali, Eid, Independence Day), clearly state the exclusions and minimum billing amounts.
  • Automated Post-Service Feedback: Use tools like ReviewBoostPRO to send an automated WhatsApp message right after a customer completes their transaction or appointment. This captures unhappy feelings privately before they spill over onto public review platforms.
💡 Pro-Tip for Local Businesses Instead of offering flat cash discounts that eat into your profit margins, offer value-add bundles. For instance, a pharmacy or clinic can offer a free consultation or a complementary health check-up kit instead of cutting service fees. This preserves your brand value while satisfying the customer's urge for a "deal."

5. Turning Price-Sensitive Critics Into Loyal Brand Advocates

It sounds counterintuitive, but a customer who complains about pricing often cares deeply about your business—otherwise, they wouldn't bother writing a review or speaking up. They wanted your service badly enough to walk through your doors, but felt the math didn't add up in their favor.

By responding with grace, offering a personalized loyalty perk via WhatsApp, and demonstrating that you value their patronage, you can frequently convert a complaining critic into your most vocal local promoter. When prospective clients read a review thread where a business handled a pricing complaint with utmost maturity, it builds instant trust.

Remember, maintaining a pristine online reputation isn't about hiding negative feedback; it is about showcasing how brilliantly your local business resolves it.
