Can You Edit a Google Review Response After Posting It?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 14, 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this scenario: It is a busy Saturday evening at your dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai, or your multi-specialty restaurant in Koramangala, Bangalore. You receive a glowing 5-star review from a happy customer. Eager to show your appreciation and boost your local SEO ranking, you quickly type out a warm thank-you message on your Google Business Profile dashboard and hit "Post."

An hour later, when things finally quiet down, you check the live review page on Google Maps. To your horror, you notice a glaring auto-correct typo—or worse, you accidentally replied to the wrong patient, or forgot to mention your upcoming festive discount offer!

Panic sets in. Can you actually edit a Google review response after you've already published it? Or are you locked into your mistake forever?

The short answer is: Yes, absolutely. Google allows business owners to edit and update their review responses at any time. Whether you spotted a spelling mistake, want to refine your tone, or need to add more details, fixing your response is quick and straightforward.

How to Edit Your Google Review Response on Desktop

Updating a published response through a desktop computer or laptop takes less than a minute. If you manage your Google Business Profile via web browser, follow these simple steps:

  • Step 1: Log in to the Google account associated with your verified Google Business Profile.
  • Step 2: Go to Google Search and type your business name and city (e.g., "Gupta Auto Repair Mumbai" or "Aura Salon Pune") directly into the search bar, or go straight to business.google.com.
  • Step 3: On your Business Profile knowledge panel displayed right on the search results page, click on "Read reviews" or navigate to the "Reviews" tab.
  • Step 4: Scroll through the list of customer reviews until you find the specific review and response you want to change.
  • Step 5: Click on the three vertical dots (the menu icon) located next to your existing response.
  • Step 6: Select "Edit" from the dropdown menu, modify your text to perfection, and click "Post" to save your changes instantly.
Pro Tip for Local Business Owners When you edit a response, Google updates the text immediately. There is no waiting period or re-verification required, making it effortless to keep your public communication polished and professional.

How to Edit Your Review Response Using a Smartphone

As an Indian business owner—whether you run a busy pharmacy in Connaught Place, New Delhi, or a boutique hotel in Jaipur—you are likely managing your day-to-day operations entirely from your mobile phone. Fortunately, Google makes mobile management equally seamless.

To edit a review response directly from your Android smartphone or iPhone:

  • Open the Google Maps app or the Google Search app while logged into your business account.
  • Search for your business name to bring up your Business Profile.
  • Tap on your business profile dashboard, then tap on "Reviews".
  • Locate the review containing the response you wish to update.
  • Tap the menu options on your reply, select Edit, type your revised message, and hit submit.

Best Practices for Crafting the Perfect Review Response

Since you now know that you *can* edit your responses, you shouldn't rely on edits to fix careless errors every time. Getting your response right the first time establishes authority. Keep these rules in mind:

  • Personalize Every Reply: Avoid generic copy-paste templates. Mention the customer's name and specific details of their visit (e.g., "Thanks for visiting our pharmacy in T. Nagar, Priya! Glad we had your prescribed medicines in stock.").
  • Incorporate Local Keywords Naturally: Mentioning your neighborhood or service keywords subtly helps local SEO. For instance: "Thank you for choosing our auto repair workshop in Andheri West, Amit!"
  • Handle Negative Reviews Calmly: If you receive a critical review, never reply defensively. Acknowledge their concern, apologize for any shortcoming, and take the conversation offline by providing a phone number or email address.
Important Limitation to Keep in Mind Remember that while you can edit your own responses as a business owner, you cannot edit the actual review written by the customer. If a customer leaves incorrect information in their review, your only recourse is to reply professionally clarifying the facts or requesting them to update it, or flagging it for removal if it violates Google’s content guidelines.
