What Should I Reply to a Review That Contains Personal Information?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 24, 2026 • 7 min read

Imagine logging into your Google Business Profile for your dental clinic in Mumbai or your auto repair shop in Bangalore, only to find a new review. Instead of a standard comment about your service, the customer has posted their full phone number, home address, specific medical diagnosis, or a private transaction ID.

Suddenly, you are faced with a delicate dilemma. Do you reply publicly? Do you delete it? How do you protect your customer's privacy while ensuring your business reputation remains intact?

Handling reviews that contain Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is a critical task for local business owners in India. With the implementation of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, managing customer data responsibly is no longer just good practice—it is a legal necessity.

Why Personal Information Appears in Google Reviews

Most customers do not intentionally expose their private details. They usually fall into a few common categories when writing public feedback:

  • Misunderstanding the platform: Many elderly or tech-inexperienced users believe they are filling out a private customer support form or a direct messaging window rather than a public forum.
  • Frustration and urgency: A customer facing an emergency car breakdown in Delhi or a delayed pharmacy delivery in Chennai might paste their direct mobile number in all-caps, demanding an immediate callback.
  • Over-sharing medical or financial data: Patients at diagnostic centers or clinics sometimes inadvertently mention specific treatments, prescription dosages, or billing details in an attempt to prove their identity.
⚠️ Immediate Danger Alert Leaving a customer's phone number, email, or sensitive health data publicly visible on your Google listing invites unsolicited spam calls, privacy breaches, and potential liability under Indian data protection regulations.

Step 1: Do NOT Repeat or Validate the Private Data

Your absolute first rule when crafting a reply is simple: Never repeat the personal information in your response.

If a user writes, "Call me urgently at 9876543210 regarding my bill," do not reply with, "Dear Rahul, we have noted your number 9876543210..." Doing so permanently anchors that sensitive data to your business response, even if the user later edits their original review.

Step 2: Post a Neutral, Professional Public Reply

Your public reply should be brief, polite, and immediately steer the conversation to a secure, private channel. Keep it professional so that future prospective customers browsing your Mumbai salon or Pune restaurant see that you are responsive and security-conscious.

Here is an ideal template you can adapt:

"Thank you for reaching out. To protect your privacy and ensure we resolve this matter securely, we kindly ask you to edit your review to remove personal details. Please connect with our team directly via our official helpline or WhatsApp so we can assist you right away."

Step 3: Move the Conversation to a Secure Channel

Once you have posted a safe, neutral public reply, you need to handle the customer's actual issue privately. By shifting the dialogue to a direct messaging environment, you can safely collect any necessary order numbers, phone numbers, or appointment references without violating privacy norms.

Step 4: Report the Review to Google

Google’s Content Policy explicitly prohibits the sharing of personally identifiable information (PII). Once you have secured the customer's issue offline, you should request Google to remove or redact the review.

Follow these steps to report the review:

  • Locate the review on your Google Business Profile dashboard or Google Maps.
  • Click or tap the three dots next to the review.
  • Select "Report review" or "Flag as inappropriate".
  • Choose the violation category: "Personal information" or "Privacy violation".

Google typically reviews and removes policy-violating content within 24 to 72 hours.

Final Thoughts

Encountering personal information in a public Google review can feel alarming, but staying calm and methodical will protect both your customer and your business. Never repeat the sensitive data, post a polite public redirect, move the conversation to a secure platform like WhatsApp, and flag the review to Google immediately.
