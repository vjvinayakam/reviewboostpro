Method 1: Setting Up Official Google Business Profile Email Alerts

The most basic way to get notified is directly through Google. If you manage your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business), you can configure your notification settings so Google sends an email to your registered inbox whenever a new review is posted.

Here is how you can enable them:

  • Log in to your Google Business Profile dashboard using your business Gmail account.
  • Click on the three-dot menu icon in the top right corner and select Business Profile settings.
  • Click on Notifications.
  • Under the Customer reviews section, toggle the switch to turn on alerts for New reviews and Customer updates.
Limitation Alert While native Google email alerts are free, they are notorious for being delayed. Sometimes Google bundles multiple notifications into a single digest email sent days later, or worse, important alerts land straight in your Gmail Promotions or Spam folder, defeating the purpose of real-time monitoring.

Method 2: Using the Google Business Profile Mobile App

Another official route is downloading the Google Business Profile management app on your smartphone (available for both Android and iOS). Ensure push notifications are enabled in your phone's system settings for the app. Whenever a new review is submitted, a native push notification will pop up on your lock screen.

While this is an improvement over email, it still requires your staff or managers to keep signed into the Google app on their personal or work phones, which can lead to missed notifications during shift changes or busy operational hours.

Best Practices for Responding Once You Get Notified

Getting the notification is only half the battle. How you reply dictates how prospective customers perceive your brand. Follow these simple rules when crafting your response:

For Positive Reviews: Do not just say "Thanks." Personalise it! Mention the service they received (e.g., "Hi Rajesh, glad you loved your root canal treatment at our clinic in Hyderabad! Keep smiling."). This adds authentic local flavor and helps SEO.

For Negative Reviews: Stay calm, professional, and empathetic. Acknowledge their frustration, apologize for the shortfall, and take the conversation offline by providing a phone number or email address (e.g., "We are sorry your experience at our auto repair shop fell short, Amit. Please call our manager directly at +91-98765-XXXXX so we can make it right.").
