What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Complains About Price?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Running a local business in India means mastering a unique art form: negotiating, building trust, and convincing customers that quality always outweighs a cheap bargain. Whether you manage a dental clinic in Andheri, Mumbai, a fine-dining South Indian restaurant in T. Nagar, Chennai, or a premier car repair workshop in Koramangala, Bangalore, you have undoubtedly heard this phrase:

"Bhaiya, thoda kam karo na, dusri jagah sasta mil raha hai!" (Brother, lower the price a bit, it's cheaper elsewhere!)

Price complaints are an everyday reality for Indian business owners. However, how you handle these complaints—especially when they transform into public 1-star Google reviews—can make or break your local reputation. In a hyper-competitive market where prospective clients read online reviews before stepping foot in your establishment, ignoring or rudely dismissing a price complaint is a recipe for disaster.

Let's dive into the psychology of Indian consumers regarding pricing, examine the best templates for replying to price complaints online, and discover how proactive review management can completely protect your brand.

Why Indian Consumers Complain About Price (The Psychology)

In India, value seeking is deeply ingrained. From street-side sabzi mandis to air-conditioned supermarkets, haggling is a cultural default. When a customer walks into your pharmacy in Delhi or books a bridal makeup session at your salon in Pune, they aren't necessarily broke—they want reassurance that they are getting their hard-earned money's worth.

When a customer leaves a negative review complaining that your services or products are "too expensive" or "overpriced," they are signaling one of two things:

  • A Mismatch in Expectations: They did not understand what was included in your ₹2,000 service fee versus a competitor's ₹1,200 fee (such as branded materials, expert technicians, or post-service warranty).
  • An Emotional Reaction: They had an unpleasant checkout experience or felt pressured, leading them to weaponize their wallet online through a harsh Google review.
The Danger of Defensive Replies Never reply to a price complaint with defensive statements like, "If you want cheap quality, go somewhere else!" or "Our prices are fixed." This immediately alienates future readers who perceive your business as arrogant and unapproachable.

How to Craft the Perfect Public Reply to a Price Complaint

When a price complaint lands on your Google Business Profile, your response is not meant for the angry customer alone. It is a billboard viewed by hundreds of potential local customers daily. Your goals are to show empathy, reframe your value proposition, and move the conversation offline.

Here is a proven framework you can adapt:

  • Step 1: Acknowledge and Validate: Thank them for taking the time to share feedback, even if it is critical.
  • Step 2: Highlight Value & Quality: Gently remind readers of what justifies your pricing—certified ingredients, genuine spare parts, expert doctors, or premium ambiance.
  • Step 3: Invite Offline Dialogue: Provide a direct phone number or WhatsApp contact to discuss their concerns personally.

Real-World Response Templates for Different Businesses

Different industries require different tones. Here are ready-to-use templates tailored for popular Indian local businesses:

1. For Clinics & Healthcare Providers (Mumbai, Delhi, etc.)

"Dear [Name], thank you for sharing your feedback. At our clinic, we utilize USFDA-approved equipment and maintain strict sterilization protocols overseen by senior specialists. We understand that healthcare costs are an important consideration, and we would love to explain our treatment breakdown to you personally. Please connect with our clinic manager at [Phone Number]."

2. For Restaurants & Cafes (Bangalore, Hyderabad, etc.)

"Hi [Name], thanks for dining with us. We source our spices directly from native farms and use 100% pure ghee and premium ingredients to ensure authentic taste and safety. We are sorry if our pricing felt steep for your experience today. Please drop us a message on WhatsApp at [Number] so we can make it up to you on your next visit!"

3. For Salons & Spas (Pune, Chennai, etc.)

"Hello [Name], thank you for visiting our salon. Our pricing reflects the expertise of our senior stylists and the use of authentic, sulphate-free international hair products that protect your hair health long-term. We’d love to share our exclusive membership packages with you that offer great value. Reach us at [Phone Number]!"

How to Prevent Price Complaints Before They Hit Google

The best way to handle a price complaint is to prevent it from turning into a public 1-star review in the first place. Transparency and clear communication at the point of sale solve 90% of pricing grievances.

Before beginning any service or handing over a bill:

  • Give Upfront Estimates: Never surprise an Indian customer with hidden taxes, service charges, or extra delivery fees at the billing counter.
  • Bundle the Benefits: Instead of saying "Root canal costs ₹5,000," say "Our ₹5,000 package includes painless computerized anesthesia, digital X-rays, and a 2-year warranty."
  • Leverage Immediate Feedback Loops: This is where modern tools change the game. By utilizing ReviewBoostPRO, local business owners can trigger automated WhatsApp messages right after checkout. When a customer pays, they receive a friendly WhatsApp prompt asking about their experience.

If a customer feels hesitant about the price, they often voice it right there on WhatsApp to the business owner directly, rather than storm off to Google Maps to leave a damaging public review. This gives your staff an immediate window to resolve the issue, offer a discount coupon, or explain your superior quality.
