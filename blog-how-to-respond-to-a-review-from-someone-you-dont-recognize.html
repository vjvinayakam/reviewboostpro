How to Respond to a Google Review From Someone You Don't Recognize

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 14, 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this: You run a busy dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai, or perhaps a popular family restaurant in Indiranagar, Bangalore. You open your Google Business Profile dashboard to check your latest metrics, and you spot a brand-new review. But as you scan the name—say, "Rahul Sharma" or "Priya Verma"—your mind draws a blank. You check your appointment registry, billing software, and customer ledger for the past month. Nothing. No record whatsoever.

Whether it is a glowing 5-star rating with no text or a scathing 1-star review complaining about terrible service that never happened, getting a review from an unrecognized person is a frustrating rite of passage for local business owners across India. How you handle this public feedback can make or break your local reputation.

Why Do Unrecognized Reviews Happen?

Before rushing to flag or reply angrily, it helps to understand why these mysterious reviews land on your Google listing in the first place. Knowing the root cause helps you draft the appropriate response strategy.

  • Alias and Nicknames: Many Indian consumers use online pseudonyms, shortened names, or their middle names on their Google accounts, which rarely match the formal name they give at your billing counter or clinic reception desk.
  • Family Members Visiting on Behalf: A husband might leave a review for an auto repair shop in Pune that his wife actually visited, or a son might review a pharmacy in Delhi where his elderly mother bought medicines.
  • Mistaken Identity: Customers frequently confuse business names, especially in crowded commercial hubs like Connaught Place in Delhi or T. Nagar in Chennai, accidentally leaving feedback for your store when they intended to review the shop next door.
  • Spam and Competitor Sabotage: Unfortunately, fake negative reviews from bot accounts or unscrupulous local competitors trying to drag down your ratings do happen.
  • Genuine Unresolved Experiences: On rare occasions, a walk-in customer had a poor experience, but because your billing system was informal or busy, their data was never captured.
⚠️ The Danger of Ignoring Them Leaving an unrecognized negative review unanswered signals to prospective customers reading your profile that you do not care about client feedback. Worse, Google’s algorithm values engagement; replying promptly demonstrates active management of your online presence.

The Step-by-Step Response Framework

When you spot a review from an unknown user, never lash out defensively. Potential customers across India are watching how you treat strangers and patrons alike. Follow this structured framework to address the situation with poise and professionalism.

Step 1: Deep Dive Into Your Internal Records

Start by doing a thorough internal audit before writing a single word of your reply. Check your appointment apps, point-of-sale (POS) software, WhatsApp chat histories, and physical ledgers. Look beyond exact name matches—search by phone number if available, date of visit, or service mentioned.

If you run a service business like a salon in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, or an auto repair garage, search for the vehicle registration number or specific treatment mentioned in the review text. Often, a quick search reveals that "Rahul" is actually the son of a regular client who visited last Tuesday.

Step 2: Write a Professional, Open-Ended Response

If your internal search turns up empty, your public response should remain calm, courteous, and inquisitive. You want to show future readers that you are diligent while inviting the reviewer to connect offline to clear up the confusion.

Here is a template you can adapt for your business:

"Hello! We pride ourselves on providing top-tier service at our establishment, but we couldn't find any record of your visit under this name in our system. We would love to look into this immediately and make things right if there was a mix-up. Could you please reach out to our manager directly at our clinic/store number or via WhatsApp so we can verify your details?" — Standard Professional Response Template

This approach protects your brand image. If it is an honest mistake or a real customer under a pseudonym, they will usually reply or update their review. If it is spam, prospective customers reading your response will see that you acted professionally and tried to help.

When and How to Flag Fake Reviews

If the unrecognized review is clearly abusive, spammy, contains hate speech, or comes from a known local competitor violating Google’s Terms of Service, do not engage in a public argument. Instead, use Google’s reporting mechanism.

  • Navigate to your Google Business Profile dashboard and locate the specific review.
  • Click on the three vertical dots next to the review and select "Report review".
  • Choose the violation category that fits best (e.g., Spam, Conflict of Interest, or Harassment).
  • Monitor your Google support ticket status over the next few days. If Google refuses to remove it, your polite, professional public reply remains your best defense.

Pro Tips for Indian Local Businesses

Managing online reputation in India requires local context. Keep these practical tips in mind:

  • Encourage Full Names Nicely: Train your front-desk staff or billing executives to politely ask customers for their preferred Google profile name if they sign up for a loyalty program or review incentive.
  • Leverage WhatsApp: Since nearly all Indian consumers use WhatsApp daily, sending review links via WhatsApp yields much higher verified response rates than traditional SMS or email ever could.
  • Keep Responses Prompt: Try to reply to all reviews—recognized or not—within 24 hours. Google rewards active profile management with better local pack visibility.
