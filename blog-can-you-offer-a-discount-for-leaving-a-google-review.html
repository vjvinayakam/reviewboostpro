Can You Offer a Discount for Leaving a Google Review?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 14, 2026 • 8 min read

Picture this scenario: A customer walks into your trendy salon in Bandra, Mumbai, or your family-friendly dental clinic in Indiranagar, Bangalore. They rave about your service, smiling broadly. You think to yourself, "If I just offer them ₹100 off today's bill, or a free dental consultation voucher, they'll whip out their phone and leave me a five-star Google review right on the spot."

It sounds like an absolute no-brainer for local business growth. After all, everyone loves a discount, and your business desperately needs those glowing reviews to climb the local Google Maps pack. But before you print out tent cards promising a flat 10% discount in exchange for feedback, you need to pause.

Can you legally and policy-wise offer a discount for a Google review? The short answer is a strict no. Doing so violates Google’s terms of service and can severely damage your local search ranking. However, there is a world of difference between buying reviews and ethically encouraging them. Let’s break down what Google allows, what it punishes, and how Indian business owners can legally scale their reviews without risking their Google Business Profile.

What Google’s Official Policy Says About Incentivized Reviews

Google has very clear guidelines regarding conflicts of interest. According to Google’s Maps User Generated Content Policy, reviews must reflect unbiased, genuine customer experiences. If money, discounts, freebies, or gifts change hands in exchange for a review, that review is immediately classified as biased.

Google’s automated filters and human moderators look for specific red flags. If your customers write reviews mentioning phrases like "Got a 10% discount for writing this" or "Free consultation for a five-star rating," Google’s algorithm flags it instantly. Furthermore, if Google suspects systematic incentivization, the consequences can be devastating:

  • Bulk Review Deletion: Google can wipe out dozens or hundreds of your hard-earned reviews overnight without warning.
  • Review Suspension: Google may temporarily or permanently disable the review feature on your Google Business Profile, preventing customers from leaving any future feedback.
  • Profile Suspension: In severe cases of policy violations, Google can suspend your entire business listing, making you completely invisible on Google Maps and local search results in cities like Delhi, Pune, Chennai, or Hyderabad.
⚠️ The Risk is Real Many local pharmacies and auto repair shops across India try offering small cashbacks or free samples for reviews thinking Google won't notice. But with modern machine learning, Google’s review filters catch unnatural spikes in five-star reviews correlated with promotional offers almost immediately.

Can You Offer Incentives Only for Positive Reviews? (The Worst Mistake)

Some business owners try to find a clever loophole: they offer a discount exclusively for five-star reviews, or they tell customers at the counter, "Leave us a good review and show me to get ₹50 off."

This is an even graver violation known as review gating or selective solicitation. Google explicitly prohibits businesses from cherry-picking happy customers or financially rewarding only positive feedback while burying negative experiences. If competitors flag your profile for offering conditional discounts, your listing faces immediate scrutiny and potential penalties.

What CAN You Do? Ethical Ways to Encourage Reviews in India

Just because you cannot pay or discount someone to write a review doesn’t mean you have to sit back and wait passively. There are plenty of ways to inspire your customers to share their authentic experiences without violating any guidelines.

The secret lies in timing and convenience. Customers rarely leave reviews because they are lazy or forgetful—not because they are unwilling. If you make the process frictionless, review volume naturally skyrockets.

This is where automation comes in. Instead of awkwardly asking customers for a review face-to-face while the billing queue is piling up, successful businesses across India use WhatsApp automation. WhatsApp boasts a near 98% open rate in India, making it the absolute best channel to reach customers right after they leave your store, clinic, or restaurant.

"We used to put up paper QR codes at our billing counter in Hyderabad, but barely 2 out of 50 people scanned them. When we started automating friendly WhatsApp review requests right after a patient’s appointment, our reviews jumped from 40 to over 400 in just three months without offering a single rupee of discount." — Dr. Rajesh Sharma, Chain of Dental Clinics, Hyderabad
