What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Says the Product Was Too Expensive?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 2026 • 8 min read

Every Indian business owner knows the sinking feeling of opening Google Maps or checking feedback and seeing a harsh review left by an unhappy shopper complaining: "Bhot mehenga hai," or simply, "Overpriced products and poor value for money." Whether you run a bustling dental clinic in Mumbai, a trendy fashion salon in Bangalore, an authentic restaurant in Delhi, or an auto repair shop in Pune, price complaints are bound to pop up from time to time.

In the highly price-conscious Indian market, value is everything. Customers want the best quality, but they also want a bargain. When someone leaves a review stating your product or service was too expensive, it doesn't just sting—it can deter prospective buyers who are browsing your Google Business Profile before deciding whether to visit you or your competitor.

How you respond to these price complaints can turn a negative public perception into a showcase of your brand's quality, transparency, and customer-first attitude. Let’s dive deep into the psychology of Indian consumers regarding pricing, practical strategies to craft the perfect reply, and ready-to-use templates for your local business.

Understanding the Root Cause of Price Complaints in India

Before typing out an emotional defense, take a step back. Why do Indian customers complain about pricing online? Often, it stems from a gap in expectations versus perceived value. In bustling commercial hubs like Chennai, Hyderabad, and Kolkata, shoppers have access to countless local vendors, online marketplaces, and street markets. They compare your premium pricing with mass-produced alternatives without initially realizing the craft, overhead, safety standards, or genuine ingredients involved.

Sometimes, a price complaint is simply an emotional reaction to an unexpected bill or service charge. When responding, your primary goal is never to argue or sound defensive. Engaging in a public argument over ₹500 or ₹2,000 makes your business look petty. Instead, treat the review as an opportunity to educate future readers about why your pricing reflects superior quality.

⚠️ What NOT to Do Never reply with phrases like "If you can't afford it, don't buy it" or "Our prices are standard, you just want cheap things." This alienates potential clients and completely ruins your professional reputation on Google Maps.

Strategy 1: Pivot Toward Quality, Safety, and Authenticity

The best way to address a price complaint is to gently pivot the conversation toward the value proposition. If a customer at your Bangalore pharmacy or Delhi clinic complains about the cost of a specialized treatment or premium wellness product, highlight the certifications, authenticity, and long-term benefits.

Indian consumers are willing to pay top rupee when they understand the safety and authenticity behind the price tag. For instance, if you run an auto repair shop in Mumbai and use genuine OEM spare parts instead of cheap local knock-offs, make sure your reply highlights durability and vehicle safety.

Example Response Template:

"Dear [Customer Name], thank you for sharing your feedback with us at [Business Name]. We completely understand that budget is an important factor when choosing a [product/service]. However, our pricing directly reflects the 100% genuine ingredients, strict safety protocols, and expert craftsmanship we invest in every single item. We never compromise on quality, ensuring you get safe and lasting results. We would love to connect with you directly to discuss how we can better meet your expectations next time!"

Strategy 2: Invite Them Offline for a Transparent Discussion

Public message boards are terrible places to negotiate pricing or debate financial details. Whenever a customer complains about expenses, your response should acknowledge their perspective briefly and immediately invite them to continue the conversation over a phone call or a private WhatsApp chat.

This shows casual readers looking at your Google profile that you are proactive, attentive, and genuinely care about customer satisfaction. Strategy 3: Highlight Hidden Inclusions and Service Standards

Often, a customer sees only the final billing amount without factoring in complementary services. If you run a salon in Pune, your hair treatment price might include premium post-wash serums, scalp massages, and professional styling consultation—perks that cheaper salons charge extra for.

Use your reply to gently educate the reviewer (and prospective clients reading the thread) about everything included in that price.

"Hi [Customer Name], thanks for visiting [Business Name] in Hyderabad. We hear your feedback regarding our pricing. We’d like to kindly remind our patrons that our package includes [mention specific freebie or high-end inclusion, e.g., free consultation, lifetime warranty, or premium organic products] which ensures maximum safety and results. We value your patronage and hope to serve you again with even greater value!"

Every business will occasionally encounter a budget-conscious customer who leaves a disgruntled note about costs. The secret to neutralizing these occasional negative reviews is maintaining an overwhelming flood of positive 5-star reviews from happy, satisfied customers who happily vouch for your value.

If you have one price complaint buried beneath fifty glowing reviews praising your exceptional quality, local shoppers will easily recognize that the complaint is an isolated incident rather than a systemic issue.

Summary Checklist for Handling Price Reviews

  • Stay Calm & Polite: Never take price complaints personally or write angry counter-arguments.
  • Focus on Value: Highlight quality, authenticity, safety standards, and skilled labor.
  • Move Offline Fast: Invite the customer to discuss the matter privately via phone or WhatsApp.
  • Build Volume: Drown out occasional cost complaints by consistently gathering fresh 5-star Google reviews using automated tools like ReviewBoostPRO.

By handling price objections with grace, professional composure, and clear communication, you turn a potential PR setback into a powerful demonstration of why your business is worth every single rupee.
