What Should I Reply to a Customer Complaining About Your Refund Policy?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 14, 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this: It is a busy Tuesday afternoon at your dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai, or your electronics repair store in Lajpat Nagar, Delhi. You open your Google Business Profile, and your heart sinks. There is a fresh 1-star review from a customer who is furious that you refused to issue a full cash refund for a service or product that clearly violated your written store policy.

In the heat of the moment, your first instinct might be to fire back angrily. You want to type out: "Read the terms and conditions before buying!" or "We clearly displayed our no-refund policy at the billing counter!"

Stop right there. When prospective customers across India—whether they are searching in Bangalore, Pune, or Hyderabad—read your Google reviews, they aren't just judging the customer who complained. They are judging how professionally you handle conflict. A harsh, defensive reply tells future clients that you are difficult to deal with. A calm, empathetic, and firm response protects your brand value.

Why Refund Policy Complaints Damage Local Businesses

Unlike national e-commerce giants that can easily absorb return shipping and instantaneous refunds of ₹500 or ₹2,000, local brick-and-mortar businesses operate on razor-thin margins. A local salon in Chennai, an auto repair garage in Ahmedabad, or an independent pharmacy cannot always offer unconditional money-back guarantees due to hygiene reasons, customized labor costs, or perishable inventory.

However, Indian consumers have grown accustomed to the flexible return policies of mega-retailers. When they face a strict store policy at a local establishment, frustration sets in, leading directly to a damaging 1-star review.

The Golden Rule of Public Replies Never argue policy details or quote paragraph numbers of your terms and conditions in a public Google review reply. Public responses are your stage to display grace, empathy, and customer care to hundreds of silent onlookers.

The 3-Step Framework for Replying to Refund Complaints

When crafting your response, you need to balance three distinct objectives: acknowledging the customer's emotional frustration, standing firm on your business ethics without sounding robotic, and steering the conversation offline immediately.

  • Step 1: Acknowledge and Validate Emotion. Start by thanking them for leaving feedback and acknowledging that they are unhappy. Do not say "We are sorry you didn't read our policy." Instead, say "We understand how frustrating it is when a service or purchase does not meet your expectations."
  • Step 2: State the Policy Politely (If Necessary). Gently mention that your policy is designed to maintain operational standards or hygiene, but keep it brief.
  • Step 3: Move to a Private Channel. Provide a direct phone number or email address so the matter can be reviewed respectfully away from the public eye.

Ready-to-Use Response Templates for Indian Business Owners

To save you time, here are three tailored templates you can adapt based on your specific situation:

Template 1: For Service Businesses (Clinics, Salons, Repair Shops)

"Hi [Customer Name], thank you for sharing your feedback with us. We always strive to give our clients in Mumbai the best experience possible, and we completely understand your disappointment regarding our service charges. Our policy is established to cover the specialized labor and materials utilized during your visit. We would love to discuss this further to see how we can assist you better. Please reach out to our manager directly at [Phone Number]."

Template 2: For Retail & Pharmacies

"Hello [Customer Name], we appreciate you bringing this to our attention. Due to safety and regulatory guidelines regarding opened packages, we are unable to process exchanges or refunds on certain items. We regret that this caused inconvenience during your visit to our store in Delhi. Please call us at [Phone Number] so we can talk through this personally."
