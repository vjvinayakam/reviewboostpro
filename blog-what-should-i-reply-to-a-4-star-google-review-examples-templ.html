What Should I Reply to a 4-Star Google Review? Examples & Templates

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 14, 2026 • 7 min read

You open your Google Business Profile dashboard, excited to see a notification. You expect a glowing 5-star rating, but instead, you spot a 4-star review. Your immediate instinct might be a twinge of disappointment. “Why not a 5? What did we miss?”

Take a deep breath. In the competitive Indian local business landscape—whether you run a busy dental clinic in Mumbai, a popular South Indian restaurant in Chennai, or a trendy hair salon in Bangalore—a 4-star review is actually a fantastic asset. It adds authenticity to your profile, shows that real customers are engaging with your establishment, and gives you actionable insights on how to achieve absolute perfection.

However, how you reply to that 4-star review can make or break your relationship with that customer and influence hundreds of prospective clients scrolling through your Google listing. In this comprehensive guide, we will explore why 4-star reviews matter, the hidden opportunities they conceal, and provide ready-to-use Indian business templates to help you craft the perfect response.

Why 4-Star Reviews Are a Hidden Blessing for Local Businesses

Consumers today are increasingly skeptical of profiles that boast a pristine, unblemished 5.0 rating across thousands of reviews. Studies show that a mix of 4.5 to 4.8 stars feels much more authentic and trustworthy. A 4-star rating tells potential customers that your business is consistently reliable, while leaving room for human imperfections.

Moreover, Google’s local search algorithm values engagement. Responding to every review—positive, neutral, or negative—signals to Google that your business is active and customer-centric. When you reply thoughtfully to a 4-star review, you show future patrons that you value feedback and are continuously improving your services.

Pro Insight for Indian Business Owners Many business owners in Delhi and Pune make the mistake of ignoring 4-star reviews because they are "good enough." Treating a 4-star review with the same enthusiasm as a 5-star review often encourages the customer to update their rating or become a loyal brand advocate.

The Golden Rules of Replying to a 4-Star Review

Before jumping into templates, let's look at the core principles of crafting a professional response:

  • Never get defensive: Even if the customer dropped a star because of something outside your control (like parking space in downtown Hyderabad), keep your tone warm, welcoming, and polite.
  • Express genuine gratitude: Thank them for taking the time out of their busy day to leave a review. Appreciation goes a long way.
  • Address the constructive feedback: If the review mentions a minor flaw (e.g., "The waiting time was a bit long"), acknowledge it gracefully without making excuses.
  • Keep it localized and personal: Use the reviewer's name and reference their specific experience rather than pasting a generic robotic reply.

Templates and Examples for Different Indian Business Types

Different industries require unique tones. Here are tailored templates designed specifically for various local businesses across India:

1. For Restaurants & Cafes (e.g., Mumbai, Bangalore)

Restaurant patrons are often vocal about food taste, ambiance, and service speed. Here is how you can handle a 4-star review about great food but slight delays during peak weekend hours:

"Namaste Priya! Thank you so much for dining at our Cafe in Bandra and leaving such kind words about our butter chicken and garlic naan. We are thrilled you loved the flavours! We noted your comment about the waiting time during Saturday rush hour and are actively working on streamlining our seating process. We look forward to serving you a 5-star experience next time!" — Rajesh Sharma, Spice & Sizzle, Mumbai

2. For Healthcare Clinics & Pharmacies (e.g., Chennai, Delhi)

In healthcare, trust and empathy are paramount. If a patient leaves 4 stars praising the doctor's diagnosis but mentioning billing queues, reply with utmost professional grace:

"Dear Dr. Ramesh, thank you for trusting our clinic for your health checkup. We are glad Dr. Gupta could assist you effectively. We sincerely apologize for the delay at the reception desk during your visit. We have recently introduced digital token systems to make your next consultation completely seamless. Wishing you good health!" — Front Desk Team, Lifeline Health Clinic, Chennai

3. For Salons & Spas (e.g., Pune, Hyderabad)

Beauty and wellness clients care deeply about styling results and pampering ambiance. Turn a 4-star haircut review into an invitation for their next styling session:

"Hi Ananya! Thank you for visiting our salon in Koregaon Park. We are delighted that you love your new hair color and layers! We appreciate your feedback regarding the background music volume—we’ll keep it more relaxing on your next visit. See you soon for your touch-up!" — Team Glow & Grace, Pune

4. For Auto Repair Shops & Service Centers (e.g., Ahmedabad, Jaipur)

Car and bike owners value transparent pricing and honest mechanics. A 4-star review here is an excellent vote of confidence in your technical work:

"Hello Vikram Ji, thank you for bringing your sedan to our service garage. We are glad our team resolved the engine noise efficiently. We take note of your suggestion about providing clearer milestone updates during servicing, and we've already updated our process. Drive safe!" — Amit Verma, Apex Auto Works, Jaipur
