How to Get More Customers From Your Google Business Profile

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 14, 2026 • 9 min read

Picture this: A potential customer in Bandra, Mumbai, is searching for a reliable dental clinic on their smartphone. They type "best dentist near me" into Google. Within a split second, Google displays the coveted Local Map Pack showing the top three clinics in the area. One clinic has 420 reviews with a 4.9-star rating. Another has 85 reviews at 4.2 stars. A third has just 12 reviews and sits at 3.8 stars.

Which clinic do you think gets the phone call or walk-in appointment? Exactly. The first one.

For local businesses across India—whether you run a dental clinic in Delhi, a trendy hair salon in Bangalore, a bustling restaurant in Pune, or an auto repair workshop in Chennai—your Google Business Profile (GBP) is no longer just a digital business card. It is your primary storefront. When optimized correctly, it acts as a round-the-clock customer acquisition engine that brings paying walk-ins and phone inquiries straight to your doorstep without spending thousands of rupees on generic ads.

In this comprehensive guide, we will walk through proven, actionable local SEO strategies to transform your Google Business Profile into a powerful customer magnet, with a heavy emphasis on the single most influential ranking factor: customer reviews.

1. Claim, Verify, and Fully Optimize Your Core Information

It sounds basic, but thousands of Indian business owners lose potential foot traffic daily simply because their core information is incomplete or inaccurate. Start by claiming your listing via Google Maps or the Google Business Profile dashboard and ensure your verification postcard or video verification is complete.

Once verified, optimize every single field:

  • Business Name: Use your actual registered business name. Do not stuff keywords like "Best Dentist in Andheri East Mumbai" unless that is your legal trade name, as Google's automated filters can suspend keyword-stuffed listings.
  • Primary & Secondary Categories: Choose your primary category with extreme care. If you run a multi-specialty healthcare practice, choose "Dental Clinic" or "Pediatrician" rather than a vague category like "Medical Center". Add relevant secondary categories like "Cosmetic Dentist" or "Emergency Dental Service".
  • Exact Address & Service Areas: Ensure your pin on Google Maps is placed precisely at your entrance. If you are a plumber in Hyderabad or an electrician in Delhi who travels to customer homes, define your service areas clearly.
  • Business Hours: Keep your hours updated, especially during Indian festive seasons like Diwali, Eid, or Pongal. Nothing frustrates a customer more than walking up to a locked shop because Google said you were open.
Pro Tip for Indian Businesses Include your WhatsApp Business contact number prominently in your profile and turn on the messaging feature. Indian consumers overwhelmingly prefer instant messaging over making phone calls or waiting for email replies.

2. Weaponize Your Visuals: High-Quality Photos and Videos

Consumers buy with their eyes before they ever step foot in your establishment. Listings with active photo updates receive significantly more direction requests and website clicks than those without.

Depending on your industry, upload geotagged, well-lit photos every week:

  • Restaurants & Cafes: Mouth-watering food dishes, ambience, seating arrangements, and the kitchen prep area to build trust in hygiene.
  • Salons & Spas: Clean styling stations, shampoo areas, sanitized tools, and before-and-after makeover shots.
  • Clinics & Pharmacies: Clean waiting rooms, friendly staff, modern diagnostic equipment, and clear store signages.
  • Auto Repair Shops: Mechanics at work, high-end diagnostic gear, and happy customers receiving their serviced vehicles.

3. Dominate the Map Pack With Authentic Customer Reviews

You can have the most beautiful photos and complete business hours, but if your competitor down the road has 500 positive reviews and you only have 15, you will lose the click. Google's algorithm heavily favors listings with a high velocity of authentic, keyword-rich reviews.

However, getting Indian customers to leave a Google review organically is notoriously difficult. If you simply ask verbally at the billing counter—"Sir, please leave us a review on Google"—over 95% of customers will nod, walk out, and completely forget about it amidst the daily rush of traffic and WhatsApp notifications.

Relying on manual email follow-ups or paper QR code flyers left on tables rarely moves the needle. Customers want absolute friction-free experiences.

4. Leverage Google Posts to Announce Offers and Updates

Think of Google Posts as your free billboard right inside search results. Many business owners set up their profile once and never touch it again. By publishing weekly updates, you signal to Google's algorithm that your business is active and vibrant.

Use Google Posts to highlight:

  • Special weekend discounts or festive combo offers (e.g., "Navratri Special Glow Facial").
  • New product launches, menu updates, or newly hired specialist doctors.
  • Important announcements like extended weekend store timings.

Always include a strong Call to Action button on your posts linking directly to your WhatsApp chat or booking page.

5. Master Review Management: Respond to Every Single Review

Getting reviews is only half the battle; how you respond dictates whether future customers trust you. Google explicitly states that responding to reviews improves your local ranking and visibility.

Follow these golden rules for review management:

  • Speed is Key: Try to respond within 24 hours.
  • Personalize Positive Reviews: Don't just paste a robotic "Thanks." Mention the customer's name and what service they enjoyed. (e.g., *"Thank you for visiting us, Priya! We're thrilled you loved your new haircut at our Andheri salon."*)
  • Handle Negative Reviews Professionally: Never get defensive or emotional. Acknowledge their frustration, apologize for the shortfall, and take the conversation offline by sharing a phone number or WhatsApp link to resolve the issue. Future customers reading your response will judge your professionalism more than the stray negative review itself.
