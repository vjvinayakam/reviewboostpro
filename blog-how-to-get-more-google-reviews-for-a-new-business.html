How to Get More Google Reviews for a New Business in India

RB
By ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Opening a new business in a bustling Indian market—whether it is a chic dental clinic in South Mumbai, a modern neighborhood salon in Bangalore's Indiranagar, or a multi-brand auto repair garage in Pune—is an exhilarating milestone. You have invested your savings, leased the perfect location, hired skilled staff, and stocked up on inventory. But as the metal shutters roll up on day one, you face a quiet, invisible hurdle that can make or break your local footfall: zero Google reviews.

In today's digital-first India, consumers do not just walk into an unfamiliar storefront on blind faith. When a potential customer in Delhi needs urgent dental care or a family in Hyderabad wants to try a new weekend dinner spot, their immediate reflex is simple: they open Google Maps, type in the service, and look at the star ratings. A new business with a blank profile or a lonely 1-star rating instantly loses out to established competitors down the road, regardless of how superior your actual service quality might be.

Getting those crucial first 50 Google reviews can feel like trying to start a motorcycle with a dead battery. Traditional methods like handing out paper feedback slips or sending generic SMS text messages rarely work anymore. Customers are busy, distracted, and prone to forgetting the moment they step outside your door. To build immediate local trust, you need a frictionless, automated strategy tailored to modern Indian consumer habits.

1. Claim and Optimize Your Google Business Profile (GBP)

Before you ask anyone for a review, your foundation must be completely solid. Your Google Business Profile is your digital storefront. If it is incomplete, customers will not even know your exact operating hours, your exact location pins, or your core service offerings.

Ensure your business name is clean and accurate without stuffing unnecessary keywords that can trigger a Google suspension. Add high-resolution photos of your physical shopfront, interior ambience, team members, and popular products or services. Indian consumers love visual confirmation; seeing a clean, well-lit clinic or a hygienic restaurant kitchen builds immediate psychological safety before they even visit.

Pro Tip for Local Indian SEO Make sure your business category is laser-focused. For instance, instead of just choosing "Doctor", select "Pediatrician" or "Dental Clinic". This ensures you show up precisely when high-intent local searchers query Google Maps in your immediate pin code.

2. Strike While the Iron is Hot: Timing is Everything

The single biggest mistake new business owners make is asking for reviews too late. If a customer visits your pharmacy in Chennai, buys their prescribed medicines, goes home, and unpacks their grocery bag, the moment of emotional connection is gone forever. You are now just a distant memory.

You need to capture feedback when the customer's satisfaction is at its absolute peak:

  • For Salons & Spas: Right when the customer looks at their fresh haircut or glowing facial in the mirror before stepping up to the billing counter.
  • For Restaurants: Just as they finish their meal and express appreciation to the waiter or pay their bill via UPI.
  • For Clinics & Pharmacies: As the consultation concludes and the patient feels reassured by the doctor's diagnosis and empathy.
  • For Auto Repair Shops: When you hand over the keys to a freshly serviced, spotless vehicle that runs smoothly.

3. Move Past Paper and SMS: The Power of WhatsApp in India

Let's be honest about consumer behavior in India: nobody checks their traditional SMS inbox anymore except to read bank transaction alerts and spam promotional codes. Paper feedback forms end up crumpled at the bottom of handbags or tossed into dustbins.

If you want a response rate above 40%, you must meet your customers where they live, breathe, and chat every single day: WhatsApp. With over 500 million active users in India, WhatsApp is the undisputed king of digital communication. A personalized WhatsApp message feels warm, direct, and intimate compared to a cold email or automated robocall.

This is precisely why smart new businesses use tools like ReviewBoostPRO. A gentle, personalized greeting lands on the customer's phone with a direct one-click link straight to your Google review page, eliminating every possible point of friction.

Avoid Review Gating Pitfalls Never incentivize reviews by offering cash discounts, free items, or lottery entries in exchange for a 5-star rating. Google's strict automated filters can penalize or wipe out reviews obtained through obvious bribery. Instead, rely on exceptional service combined with polite, perfectly timed reminders.

4. Train Your Frontline Staff to Drive the Request

Your software tools and marketing strategies are only as good as the human beings interacting with your customers every single day. Your receptionist, cashier, floor manager, or service advisor must become your primary brand ambassadors.

Give your team a simple script. For example, your clinic receptionist can smile and say: "Sir/Ma'am, we just opened our clinic here in Bangalore to serve our neighborhood better. If Dr. Sharma helped you today, I'm sending a quick WhatsApp message to your phone. A 2-word review on Google helps other local families find us easily!"

When staff understand that positive reviews build the reputation of the very workplace that pays their salary, their enthusiasm becomes genuine and infectious.

"When we launched our multi-cuisine restaurant in Pune, we struggled for two months to cross 10 reviews. Customers loved our food, but they simply forgot to post online. Once we integrated ReviewBoostPRO at our billing counter via automated WhatsApp prompts, our review count jumped from 12 to 140 in less than 45 days. Our weekend walk-ins have doubled because we now rank in the top 3 on Google Maps!" — Rajesh Deshmukh, Owner, Spice & Sizzle Bistro, Pune

5. Respond to Every Single Review Promptly

Getting reviews is only half the battle; how you respond to them dictates your brand image to future prospects. When a customer takes two minutes out of their busy day to write a review about your new business, acknowledging them is non-negotiable.

For positive reviews, avoid generic copy-paste templates like "Thanks for the review." Instead, personalize it by mentioning their name and specific details: "Thank you so much, Priya! We are thrilled you loved the hair styling session at our salon in Andheri. See you next month!"

What about negative reviews? Do not panic or reply defensively. Treat constructive criticism as free business consulting. Reply politely, apologize for the shortfall, and take the conversation offline by providing a phone number to resolve their grievance. Prospective customers often judge a business more by how gracefully it handles criticism than by its flawless 5-star streak.
