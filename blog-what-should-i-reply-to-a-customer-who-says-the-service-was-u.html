What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Says the Service Was Unprofessional?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this: You run a bustling dental clinic in South Mumbai or a popular salon in Bandra. You pride yourself on top-notch customer hospitality, premium equipment, and well-trained staff. Then, a notification pops up on your Google Business Profile. A one-star review staring right back at you, featuring the dreaded words: "Extremely unprofessional service."

Your blood pressure spikes. You want to type back immediately, defend your receptionist, or explain that the clinic was overwhelmingly crowded because three emergency patients walked in simultaneously. But pause. Hitting back in anger on a public forum is the fastest way to turn a minor hiccup into a viral PR nightmare.

For local businesses across India—from diagnostic labs in Delhi to auto repair garages in Pune—how you respond to a complaint about "unprofessionalism" dictates whether prospective customers walk through your doors or scroll past to your competitor.

Why "Unprofessional Service" Reviews Hurt the Most

Unlike a complaint about a delayed order or a faulty product part, an accusation of unprofessionalism targets your character, your staff's behavior, and the core culture of your establishment. It might refer to a receptionist who spoke rudely, a technician who showed up late to a home service call in Bangalore, or a doctor who seemed rushed and dismissive.

When prospective clients read this review, they aren't just looking at the mistake; they are watching how you handle accountability. A defensive reply signals arrogance. A calm, structured, empathetic reply shows maturity, leadership, and a deep commitment to customer satisfaction.

⚠️ The Golden Rule of Negative Reviews Never argue the facts of a negative review publicly. Even if the customer is mistaken or exaggerating, a public shouting match makes your business look petty and difficult to deal with.

The 4-Step Framework for Responding to Unprofessionalism

When drafting your reply, follow a precise psychological structure that de-escalates tension and reassures anyone else reading the thread:

  • Acknowledge and Validate: Start by thanking them for bringing the matter to light and validating that their experience fell short of your standards.
  • Take Ownership (Without Making Excuses): Avoid phrases like "We were short-staffed" or "It was a festival rush." Instead, focus on the gap in their personal experience.
  • Take the Conversation Offline: Provide a direct phone number or email address so you can investigate the root cause privately and offer a resolution (like a refund, a complimentary session, or a personal apology from the manager).
  • Follow Through and Update: Once resolved, if the customer is satisfied, you can politely ask if they would consider updating their review.

Ready-to-Use Templates for Indian Business Owners

Depending on your exact industry and scenario, you can customize these proven response templates:

Template 1: For Clinics and Healthcare Centers
"Dear [Customer Name], thank you for sharing your feedback. We hold ourselves to the highest standards of patient care and empathy at our clinic, and I am deeply sorry to hear that your recent visit felt rushed and unprofessional. This is certainly not the standard of care we strive to deliver. I would appreciate the opportunity to speak with you personally to understand what went wrong. Please reach out to me directly at [Phone Number] or [Email]. Sincerely, Dr. [Name], Clinic Director."

Template 2: For Salons, Spas, and Hospitality
"Hi [Customer Name], thank you for bringing this to our attention. We pride ourselves on providing a relaxing, courteous environment, and it is clear we missed the mark during your appointment. I sincerely apologize for the lack of professionalism you experienced. We take team conduct very seriously here. Please connect with me at [Phone Number] so I can make this right for you. Warm regards, [Owner Name]."
