How to Get Google Reviews for a Business With No Customers Yet

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Launching a new business in a bustling Indian market is an exhilarating milestone. Whether you are opening a chic dental clinic in Andheri, Mumbai, launching a cozy South Indian restaurant in Koramangala, Bangalore, or setting up a modern pharmacy in Delhi, the excitement is palpable. You have invested your hard-earned savings, decorated your store, and perfected your service offerings. But then reality sets in: your Google Business Profile is sitting at zero reviews.

In today's digital-first economy, consumers rely heavily on social proof. When someone searches for a "dermatologist near me" or a "best salon in Pune," Google's local pack prioritizes businesses with a high volume of positive reviews. But how do you break the chicken-and-egg cycle? How do you attract customers when you have no reviews, and how do you get reviews when you have no customers?

Building a stellar local reputation from scratch requires strategic planning, compliance with Google's guidelines, and leveraging your immediate network. Let us dive into the ultimate guide on how to kickstart your Google Business Profile reviews when your customer registry is completely blank.

1. Optimize Your Google Business Profile Completely First

Before you ask a single person for a review, your digital storefront must look professional and welcoming. Think of your Google Business Profile as your physical storefront—you wouldn't invite guests into an empty shop with boxes everywhere.

Ensure that every single field on your dashboard is meticulously filled out. This includes your exact business category (e.g., "Auto Repair Shop" rather than just "Mechanic"), operating hours, complete address with a pin dropped accurately on the map, phone number, and website link.

Pro Tip for Indian Businesses Add high-resolution photos of your interior, exterior, team members, and products. Local customers in cities like Hyderabad and Chennai heavily judge the credibility of a business based on clear, well-lit photos before deciding to visit.

2. Tap Into Your Warm Network (Friends, Family, and Past Colleagues)

When you have zero actual paying customers, your very first batch of reviews must come from your personal and professional network. These are people who already trust you, believe in your vision, and want to see your business succeed.

Reach out to former colleagues, college batchmates, close friends, and family members living in your city. Explain that you have just launched your venture and that authentic early feedback will help your listing appear in local searches.

Important Rule of Compliance Never buy fake reviews from shady online agencies promising 50 reviews for ₹999. Google's sophisticated algorithms detect bulk fake reviews instantly, resulting in profile suspension or permanent bans. Keep your initial reviews strictly tied to genuine people who have physically visited your establishment or experienced your service.

3. Invite Beta Testers or Offer Opening Day Complimentary Services

If you run a service-oriented business like a salon, a diagnostic clinic, or a boutique gym, create a soft launch phase. Invite neighbors, local micro-influencers, or community members to try your services for free or at a heavily discounted rate in exchange for honest feedback.

For example, a new cafe in Bandra can host an exclusive tasting evening for 30 local food bloggers and neighborhood residents. Once they experience the hospitality, invite them right there to share their experience on Google.

4. Leverage WhatsApp for Instant, Frictionless Review Requests

Email review requests in India have abysmal open rates—often below 5%. WhatsApp, on the other hand, boasts a near 98% open rate. The moment your beta testers or early customers walk out of your door, your follow-up needs to land directly in their personal chat.

5. Provide Over-the-Top Exceptional Service to Early Visitors

Your first 10 customers are your most valuable marketing asset. Because you have no reviews to vouch for you, your service quality has to be so extraordinary that customers feel compelled to praise you online.

Go above and beyond. If you run a pharmacy in Delhi, offer free home delivery for elderly residents within a 3km radius. If you operate an auto repair shop in Chennai, include a complimentary interior vacuuming with every routine oil change. When you blow people away with unexpected value, they will gladly take two minutes to write a glowing 5-star review.

6. Respond to Every Single Review Instantly

Even when you only have two or three reviews, engagement matters. When a friend or early customer takes the time to leave feedback, reply within hours. A professional, warm response shows prospective customers browsing your profile that there is an active, attentive business owner behind the counter.

For instance: "Thank you so much, Priya! We are thrilled you loved our new boutique collection in our Bangalore store. Looking forward to welcoming you back soon!"
