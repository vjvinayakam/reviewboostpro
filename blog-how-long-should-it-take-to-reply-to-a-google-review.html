How Long Should It Take to Reply to a Google Review?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 2026 • 8 min read

Imagine walking into a bustling South Indian restaurant in Chennai or a modern dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai. You approach the reception counter, ask a question, and the staff member completely ignores you for three days before turning around to answer. Unthinkable, right? Yet, many Indian local business owners treat their Google Business Profile reviews with that exact kind of delayed silence.

In the hyper-competitive local markets of Bangalore, Delhi, Pune, and Hyderabad, consumers rely heavily on digital validation before deciding where to spend their hard-earned rupees. When a customer takes the time out of their busy day to leave feedback—whether it is a glowing 5-star review for your pharmacy or a frustrated 2-star complaint about auto repair wait times—the clock starts ticking immediately.

So, what is the golden rule for response times? How quickly do Google's algorithms and your prospective customers expect you to reply?

The Magic Window: 24 to 48 Hours

For standard, positive Google reviews, the absolute gold standard for reply time is within 24 to 48 hours. Responding within this timeframe signals to the reviewer that their patronage is genuinely valued. It also demonstrates to hundreds of prospective local customers browsing your profile that your business is active, attentive, and deeply customer-centric.

Google has explicitly stated that responding to reviews helps improve your local SEO rankings and visibility in the Local Pack. When you reply promptly, you create fresh engagement signals on your Google Business Profile. Furthermore, incorporating relevant local keywords naturally into your replies—such as mentioning your location in Koramangala or Connaught Place—can give your local search visibility an extra boost.

Pro Tip for Positive Reviews Don't just copy-paste a robotic "Thanks for the review!" Mix it up. A personalized reply like, "Hi Priya, thank you so much for visiting our salon in Jubilee Hills! We are thrilled you loved your new haircut and hope to see you again soon," builds authentic local loyalty.

The Emergency Zone: Responding to Negative Reviews

While positive reviews can comfortably wait up to a day or two, negative reviews demand immediate attention. If a customer leaves a critical review complaining about slow service at your cafe or a billing error at your clinic, the ideal response time shrinks to under 6 to 12 hours.

Why is speed so vital for negative feedback? Because an unanswered negative review acts as a flashing billboard warning prospective customers to stay away. When potential buyers see a complaint sitting there unacknowledged for days, they assume you simply do not care. Conversely, a swift, polite, and professional reply can completely neutralize the damage.

    When handling a negative review swiftly, remember to:

  • Acknowledge their frustration calmly without becoming defensive or argumentative.
  • Take the conversation offline immediately by providing a direct phone number or email address.
  • Never air sensitive operational details or argue about pricing in public view.
  • Update the review public thread once the issue is successfully resolved offline to show future readers that you take action.
The Danger of Silence Leaving a 1-star review hanging for over a week tells every future customer that your business lacks accountability. Speed is your best defense against reputational damage.

Why Local Business Owners Struggle With Timing

Most Indian entrepreneurs and managers do not intentionally ignore their Google reviews. The reality is far more mundane: you are busy managing daily operations, handling staff, serving walk-in customers, and dealing with suppliers. Logging into the Google Business Profile dashboard regularly simply slips through the cracks.

When business owners finally remember to check their listings, they are often greeted by a backlog of dozens of unread reviews spanning several weeks. At that point, replying feels like an overwhelming chore rather than a simple customer relationship task.
