How to Respond to a False Google Review Without Hurting Your Local Business

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

It is 9:30 AM on a busy Tuesday in your Mumbai dental clinic, Bangalore restaurant, or Pune auto repair garage. You open your Google Business Profile, expecting to see a glowing comment from yesterday’s happy patients or diners. Instead, you see a fresh 1-star rating with no text, or worse—a long, fabricated paragraph claiming terrible service, rude staff, or overcharging.

Your heart sinks. In India’s hyper-competitive local market, a sudden drop in your star rating can immediately turn away potential walk-ins who are comparing your shop with three others down the street. Local consumers rely heavily on Google Maps before stepping foot into a salon in Chennai, checking a pharmacy in Hyderabad, or booking a hotel in Delhi.

Dealing with false, malicious, or spam Google reviews is an unfortunate reality of running a modern storefront. However, reacting out of anger can permanently damage your brand reputation. Here is the exact step-by-step playbook Indian business owners need to follow when faced with a bogus review.

Step 1: Don't Panic and Never Lose Your Temper

When you spot a review accusing your business of something completely untrue, your first instinct is often to reply defensively or call out the reviewer angrily. Do not do this. Remember: future customers are reading your response more than they are reading the fake review itself.

If you respond professionally, calmly, and factually, any prospective client reading the thread will instantly realize the review is baseless. If you respond with hostility, insults, or caps lock, you validate the fake reviewer and look unstable to everyone else.

The Golden Rule of Public Replies Never get into a prolonged public argument in your Google Business Profile comments. State your facts clearly once, invite them to speak offline, and move on.

Step 2: Investigate Your Records (Is It Really Fake?)

Before flagging the review, do a quick internal audit. Check your appointment software, billing logs, or CCTV footage. Ask your team members in Delhi or Bangalore if they remember a customer matching that name or description.

Sometimes, legitimate customers have a bad day or misunderstand a policy. Other times, it is unmistakably a competitor trying to drag down your rating, a disgruntled ex-employee, or a prankster who has never even stepped inside your establishment.

Step 3: Draft a Professional, Public Reply

Even if Google takes weeks to process a removal request, you cannot leave a 1-star review hanging without an answer. Your response should serve two purposes: show Google that you are actively managing your listing, and reassure potential clients that you take customer service seriously.

Here are templates tailored for Indian businesses:

  • For a completely unknown person/competitor spam: "Hello [Name], we take pride in our service at [Business Name], but we cannot find any record of you visiting our clinic/store in Mumbai under this name. Please reach out to our manager at [Phone Number] with details of your visit so we can investigate immediately."
  • For an exaggeration or misunderstanding: "Hi [Name], thank you for sharing your feedback, though we are surprised by this description. Our team in Hyderabad always strives to provide transparent pricing and polite service. Please connect with us directly on WhatsApp at [Number] so we can resolve this right away."

Step 4: Formally Flag the Review to Google

Google has strict guidelines against spam, harassment, and conflict of interest (such as competitors reviewing your business). You can report reviews that violate these policies directly from your Google Business Profile dashboard or Google Maps app.

Click the three dots next to the review, select "Report review", and choose the most accurate reason (e.g., "Spam", "Conflict of interest", or "Off-topic"). Keep in mind that Google rejects removal requests unless they clearly breach terms of service, so make sure your categorization is precise.

Pro Tip for Faster Removal If a review uses abusive language, contains personal information, or is part of a coordinated attack by a competitor, it stands a much higher chance of being removed within 48 to 72 hours after reporting.

Step 5: Bury the Fake Review with Real, Authentic 5-Star Reviews

The harsh truth of local SEO is that Google's support team does not always remove fake reviews, even when they are obviously malicious. Waiting weeks for a ticket resolution can cost you thousands of rupees in lost sales.

The ultimate antidote to a single 1-star fake review is an influx of 10 genuine 5-star reviews from your actual, happy customers. When your overall rating stays strong at 4.8 stars with hundreds of reviews, a random negative outlier loses all its power.
