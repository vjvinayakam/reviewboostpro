Should You Send a Second Google Review Request? The Ultimate Guide

RB
By ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 14, 2026 • 7 min read

Imagine this scenario: A customer walks into Rajesh’s dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai, undergoes a painless root canal treatment, and leaves smiling. Rajesh’s front desk sends a polite WhatsApp message asking for a Google review right after the patient steps out. But life happens. The patient gets stuck in local train traffic, reaches home, gets busy cooking dinner, and completely forgets about the message.

Three days later, Rajesh’s Google Business Profile review count remains completely unchanged. Should Rajesh send a second request? Will it convert that busy customer into a 5-star review, or will it annoy them enough to block his clinic's WhatsApp number?

This is a dilemma faced by thousands of local business owners across India—from busy restaurants in Koramangala, Bangalore, to popular diagnostic labs in Connaught Place, New Delhi. Let’s dive deep into the data, consumer psychology, and practical rules of sending a second Google review request without looking spammy.

Why the First Review Request Isn’t Always Enough

In the bustling Indian market, consumers are constantly bombarded with notifications. Between Zomato delivery updates, bank transaction alerts on SMS, and family WhatsApp groups, your polite review request is fighting for attention in an extremely crowded digital space.

Statistics show that the average open rate for a WhatsApp review message sent immediately after service is remarkably high—often exceeding 80%. However, reading a message while walking out of a salon in Pune or stepping into a cab doesn’t mean the customer has the time to click your Google link, log in, write feedback, and hit submit.

They mean to do it later. But "later" almost always translates to "never." This is precisely why a well-timed second reminder is not just acceptable—it is essential if you want to capture the reviews your business genuinely deserves.

The Reality of Consumer Attention

Over 65% of customers who eventually leave a positive review do so after receiving a friendly, gentle follow-up message. Silence from the customer rarely means annoyance; it almost always means they simply got distracted.

The Golden Rules of Sending a Second Reminder

While sending a second request works wonders, doing it incorrectly can backfire. If you hound a customer every single day, you damage your brand reputation. To keep your review generation campaign smooth and effective, follow these foundational rules:

  • Never follow up on the same day: Give your customer at least 48 to 72 hours of breathing room before prompting them again.
  • Change the angle of your message: Your second request should feel fresh, acknowledging that they have a busy schedule rather than repeating the exact same text word-for-word.
  • Keep the friction absolute zero: Ensure your message contains a direct, single-tap link straight to your Google review submission box.
  • Stop at two: Never send a third or fourth automated follow-up. If two gentle nudges don't work, let it go gracefully.

How Timing Varies Across Different Indian Businesses

The ideal interval for your second review request depends heavily on the nature of your local business:

Clinics and Pharmacies: Patients appreciate care, but health matters can be sensitive. Sending a first message 2 hours post-visit and a second reminder after 3 days works best. By then, the medication has started working and they are feeling better.

Restaurants and Cafes: Dining is an emotional, immediate experience. If they didn't review when leaving your restaurant in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, send a gentle follow-up the next afternoon around lunchtime with a tempting food photo. Conversion rates skyrocket here.

Salons and Spas: A grooming session is relaxing. Remind them 48 hours later when they are admiring their fresh haircut or glowing skin in the mirror.

Auto Repair Shops and Hardware Stores: Car repairs take time and involve high trust. Send the first request when they pick up their vehicle, and the second request after 4 to 5 days, once they’ve test-driven the vehicle and experienced the smooth engine performance.

Compliance Warning

Always respect customer opt-outs. If a customer replies asking not to be messaged or ignores both prompts, remove them from further automated sequences immediately to comply with WhatsApp business policies.

Crafting the Perfect Second WhatsApp Message

Tone is everything in Indian customer service. Your second message should be warm, polite, and empathetic to their busy daily routine. Avoid corporate jargon entirely.

Here is a proven template you can adapt for your local business:

"Hi Priya! We know how hectic the Mumbai local commute and workday can be. Just wanted to drop a quick, gentle reminder about your visit to our clinic on Tuesday. If Dr. Sharma helped you feel better, a quick 30-second Google review would mean the world to our small team. Here is the direct link: [Link]. Thank you so much!" — Standard High-Converting WhatsApp Follow-up Template

Notice how this template acknowledges their busy schedule upfront, removes guilt, and highlights that you are a local team, which builds instant emotional connection.
