What Should I Reply to a Google Review With No Comment?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this scenario: You open your Google Business Profile dashboard for your clinic in Bandra, Mumbai or your bustling family restaurant in Koramangala, Bangalore. You see a glorious, glowing 5-star rating notification. Your heart skips a beat with satisfaction. But as you click open the review, you are met with absolute silence—just five golden stars and no written feedback, no description, and no comment.

Your immediate reaction might be: "Do I even bother replying to this? There's literally nothing to respond to!"

The short answer is a resounding yes. Ignoring empty star ratings is one of the most common mistakes local business owners make across India. Whether you run a dental clinic in Delhi, a luxury salon in Pune, or an auto repair workshop in Chennai, empty reviews carry massive weight for your local search rankings and customer trust.

Why Empty Reviews Matter Google’s algorithm values engagement and overall activity on your Business Profile. Replying to empty 5-star reviews signals to Google that your business is active, attentive, and deeply engaged with your clientele, which directly helps you rank higher in the local Map pack.

Why Do Customers Leave 5-Star Reviews With No Comments?

Before crafting your replies, it helps to understand Indian consumer behavior. Life in urban India moves at lightning speed. When a customer finishes a delicious meal at a restaurant in Connaught Place or gets a quick haircut at a salon in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, they want to reward your good service. However, typing out a long paragraph on a smartphone keyboard while dodging traffic or stepping into an auto-rickshaw feels like a chore.

Tools like ReviewBoostPRO make this process frictionless by sending a direct WhatsApp link that lets patrons tap a star rating in seconds. Because the barrier to entry is so low, many customers tap five stars and hit submit immediately without writing a single word. They showed their love—now it's your turn to acknowledge it.

The Golden Rules of Replying to No-Comment Reviews

When there is no text to reference, your reply needs to be strategic, warm, and optimized for local SEO. Keep these rules in mind:

  • Acknowledge the rating: Always thank them for the 5-star rating specifically.
  • Mention your local business name and service: Gently weave in keywords like "our dental clinic in Chennai" or "our auto repair shop in Pune" to boost local SEO.
  • Keep it concise: Don't write an essay. A warm 2-3 sentence response is more than enough.
  • Add a welcoming closing: Invite them back for their next visit or checkup.
