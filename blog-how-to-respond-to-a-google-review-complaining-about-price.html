How to Respond to a Google Review Complaining About Price

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this: You run a popular dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai, or a multi-specialty auto repair garage in Indiranagar, Bangalore. You've spent years building your reputation, investing in genuine spare parts or top-tier clinical equipment, and keeping your staff well-trained. Then, a notification pops up on your phone. A 1-star Google review appears: "Extremely expensive! Charged ₹1,500 for a simple 10-minute job. Total loot."

For Indian local business owners, price complaints can feel intensely personal and frustrating. In a price-sensitive market like India, where customers love hunting for a bargain, a negative review focusing on cost can instantly deter potential walk-ins or callers. However, how you respond to that review matters infinitely more than the review itself. Future customers aren't just looking at whether you have 5 stars—they are reading your replies to see how you handle criticism, value your clients, and justify your worth.

Why Price Complaints Hurt Local Businesses (And Why They Happen)

In cities ranging from Pune and Hyderabad to Chennai and Delhi, consumers have immense choice. Whether someone is looking for a pharmacy delivery, a luxury hair salon, or a family restaurant, comparing prices takes seconds. When a customer leaves a price-based negative review, it usually stems from one of three scenarios:

  • Mismatched Expectations: The customer did not ask about pricing beforehand or misunderstood the scope of service.
  • Value Blindness: They see only the final bill, failing to recognize the quality of ingredients, sterile environment, genuine warranty, or expert craftsmanship involved.
  • Venting Frustration: Personal financial stress or a bad day leading to taking out frustrations on an accessible digital platform.
⚠️ The Golden Rule Never argue, mock, or attack the customer in your reply. Even if they are completely unreasonable, your response is permanent public relations for your business. Speak calmly to the prospective customers reading over your shoulder.

Step-by-Step Framework for Responding to Price Reviews

Crafting the perfect response requires a delicate balance of empathy, professional boundary-setting, and value articulation. Follow this structured approach every time:

1. Acknowledge and Remain Polite: Never start by saying "You're wrong" or "Our prices are standard." Instead, thank them for sharing their feedback. It demonstrates maturity and calm leadership.

2. Reiterate Your Value Proposition: Gently remind readers what goes into your pricing. Mention things like certified professionals, high-grade materials, safety standards, or comprehensive after-service support.

3. Take the Conversation Offline: Invite the customer to call your manager or visit the store/clinic directly to discuss the bill breakdown. This shows you are proactive while removing a public pricing debate.
