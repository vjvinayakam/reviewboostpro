What Should I Reply to a Review From Someone I Don't Recognize?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 14, 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this: You walk into your clinic in Andheri, Mumbai, or open the shutters of your electronics store in Karol Bagh, Delhi. You open your Google Business Profile, sip your morning chai, and notice a brand-new review. But as you read the name—say, "Rahul Sharma" or "Priya V."—your heart sinks. You rack your brain, scroll through your appointment registers, billing software, and WhatsApp chats, but you simply cannot find a single trace of this person.

Even worse, it is a 1-star review complaining about terrible service, rude staff, or a defective product you never sold. Or perhaps, bizarrely, it's a 5-star review from someone who claims your dental clinic cured their toothache, but you're actually a car repair garage in Pune.

For local business owners across India, receiving reviews from unrecognised profiles is a common and stressful headache. Whether it's a mischievous competitor, a case of mistaken identity, a disgruntled former employee, or pure spam, how you reply matters immensely. Potential customers in Bangalore or Chennai are watching how you handle dissent. Your reply isn't just for the reviewer; it's a public performance for every future customer.

Why Do Reviews From Unknown People Happen?

Before crafting your response, it helps to understand why these mysterious reviews land on your Google Maps listing. Knowing the root cause helps you determine whether it's an honest mistake or malicious intent.

  • Pseudonyms and Nicknames: Many customers use nicknames or abbreviated names on their Google accounts (e.g., writing "Bunty" instead of their legal billing name "Bhupinder Singh").
  • Family Account Usage: A husband might post a review using his wife's Gmail account, or a teenager might leave a review using a family tablet.
  • Mistaken Business Identity: In bustling commercial hubs like Hyderabad or Chennai, adjacent shops often have similar names. A customer meant to trash the juice bar next door but accidentally tapped your pharmacy instead.
  • Competitor Sabotage: Sadly, unethical local competitors sometimes hire fake profile farms to drop 1-star ratings to sink your local SEO rankings.
  • Spam Bots and Automated Scams: Generic AI-generated comments meant to boost unrelated links or test out fake accounts.
Crucial Rule for Indian Business Owners Never panic or lash out defensively in public. Replying with anger like "We don't know you, stop lying!" makes your business look unprofessional to hundreds of prospective clients browsing your listing.

Step 1: Investigate Your Internal Records Thoroughly

Before typing a single word back, do a deep-dive investigation. Do not rely solely on your memory. Check:

  • Your booking software or physical appointment logbooks for the dates mentioned.
  • Your UPI transaction logs on Paytm, PhonePe, or Google Pay for matching names or phone numbers.
  • Your WhatsApp business chat archive. (Tools like ReviewBoostPRO keep customer communication neatly tagged, making it easy to search past interactions).
  • Ask your frontline staff, receptionist, or billing counter team if they remember any unusual interactions matching the complaint.

Step 2: Choose the Right Reply Strategy Based on Review Type

Depending on whether the unrecognised review is positive, negative, or completely irrelevant, your reply strategy must adapt.

Scenario A: A Negative Review from an Unrecognised Name

If the review is critical, your goal is twofold: show future readers that you take customer service seriously, and invite the poster to verify their identity offline so you can resolve the issue.

Sample Response Template:
"Hello [Name], thank you for sharing your feedback. However, we cannot locate any record of an appointment or transaction under your name in our [City] establishment. We take service quality very seriously and want to investigate this immediately. Could you please call us directly at [Phone Number] or drop us a message so we can verify your visit and make things right?"

Scenario B: A Positive Review from a Stranger

While a random 5-star review boosts your rating, ignoring it looks robotic, and leaning into it blindly can feel awkward. It is best to accept the appreciation graciously while keeping it professional.

Sample Response Template:
"Thank you for the wonderful rating! We always strive to provide top-notch service at our [City] location. If you are one of our valued patrons, we appreciate your support. If this was posted in error, we hope you'll drop by soon to experience our services firsthand!"

Scenario C: Clearly Spurious or Bot-Generated Reviews

If the review is completely irrelevant (e.g., praising a restaurant when you run an auto repair shop in Jaipur), it is likely spam or a misdirected review. Address it politely so readers know it's a mistake, and simultaneously flag it to Google.

Step 3: How to Flag and Report Fake Reviews to Google

If you are 100% certain the review violates Google's content policies (e.g., spam, harassment, conflict of interest, or fake identity), do not just reply—report it.

  1. Go to your Google Business Profile dashboard or Google Maps.
  2. Locate the suspicious review.
  3. Click the three vertical dots next to the review and select "Report review".
  4. Choose the reason that best fits (e.g., "Spam" or "Doesn't apply to this place").

Keep in mind that Google's automated moderation can take anywhere from a few days to a couple of weeks to remove flagged content. Having a polite, professional reply live in the meantime protects your brand image.
