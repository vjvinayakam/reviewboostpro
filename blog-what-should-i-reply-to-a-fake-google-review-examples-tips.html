What Should I Reply to a Fake Google Review? Examples & Tips

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 12, 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this: It is a busy Saturday morning at your multi-speciality dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai, or your popular family restaurant in Indiranagar, Bangalore. You open your Google Business Profile to check how things are looking, and your stomach drops. A 1-star review from someone you have never heard of, complaining about terrible service, rude staff, or food poisoning, is sitting right at the top.

You check your appointment registers, billing software, and CCTV footage. No such customer visited your establishment. It is a fake review.

For Indian business owners, a sudden influx of negative or spam reviews can cause immediate panic. Prospective customers scrolling through Google Maps judge your credibility within seconds. However, reacting out of anger or ignoring the review completely can inflict permanent damage on your local brand value.

In this guide, we will break down the exact strategies, psychological tactics, and ready-to-use templates to handle fake Google reviews professionally, protect your online reputation, and drown out malicious feedback with genuine customer experiences.

Why Do Businesses Get Fake Google Reviews?

Before crafting a reply, it helps to understand why this happens. Fake reviews are rarely random accidents. In India’s hyper-competitive local business landscape, malicious 1-star ratings usually stem from a few predictable sources:

  • Unethical Competitors: Rival businesses in your locality looking to pull down your ratings to siphon away local foot traffic.
  • Disgruntled Former Employees: Ex-staff members holding a grudge who create dummy accounts to vent frustration.
  • Pranksters & Trolls: Random internet users or bored teenagers leaving absurd claims for amusement.
  • Extortion Scams: Fraudsters who leave a 1-star review and then message your business demanding ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 via UPI to remove it.
Crucial Warning Never pay extortion money to fake reviewers. Paying once establishes you as an easy target, and they will likely return next month asking for more. Instead, report them immediately to Google.

Step 1: Don't Panic and Never Lose Your Temper

Your emotional response is precisely what the troll or competitor wants. If you reply with abuse, sarcasm, or defensive anger ("You are a liar, we never saw you here!"), future customers scrolling through your profile will view your business as unprofessional and unstable.

Remember: Your reply is not actually meant for the person who wrote the fake review. It is meant for the hundreds of genuine prospective customers in Delhi, Chennai, Pune, or Hyderabad who are reading your conversation to decide if they should trust you with their time and money.

Step 2: Take Immediate Action Behind the Scenes

Before you type out a public response, exhaust your administrative options with Google:

  • Flag the Review: Click the three dots next to the review on Google Maps or your Google Business Profile dashboard and select "Report review". Choose the appropriate violation category (e.g., Spam, Conflict of interest, or Misinformation).
  • Submit a Removal Request: If Google's automated system rejects your initial flag, visit the Google Business Profile Help Community and submit a formal removal request detailing why the review violates Google’s content policies.

Step 3: Professional Templates for Replying to Fake Reviews

While waiting for Google to review your removal request—which can take anywhere from 48 hours to a week—you must post a polite, firm public response. This signals to readers that you take customer service seriously and that the reviewer's claims are unfounded.

Here are three high-converting response templates tailored for Indian businesses:

Template A: For Complete Impersonation / "Never Visited" Claims

"Hello [Name], we take pride in our service standards at [Business Name], but we have searched our appointment/customer records for the past 6 months and cannot find any record of anyone under your name or matching this description visiting us in [City Name]. We suspect this might be a mistaken identity or a spam review. Please reach out to our manager directly at [Phone Number/Email] so we can verify your visit and resolve any genuine concerns."

Template B: Short & Fact-Oriented (Best for Trolls)

"Hello [Name], we have no record of any transaction or service rendered to you at our establishment. If you are a genuine customer of [Business Name], please call our customer support line directly with your invoice details so we can investigate this matter immediately."

Template C: The Extortion / Suspicious Account Template

"Hello, transparency and honesty are core values at [Business Name]. We have reviewed our entire daily log and found no customer interaction matching this claim. We treat all feedback very seriously, but false or malicious reviews will be reported to cyber authorities and Google support. If you visited us, please share your bill number."

Step 4: Drown Out Fake Reviews with Volume

Google’s algorithm and human shoppers care about averages, not anomalies. If your restaurant in Hyderabad has 480 positive reviews and one suspicious 1-star review, prospective diners will easily recognize that the 1-star review is an outlier.

The absolute best defense against fake reviews is an aggressive, steady stream of genuine 5-star reviews from your actual, happy customers.
