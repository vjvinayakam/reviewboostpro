What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Says They Found It Cheaper Elsewhere?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Every single day across Indian bustling marketplaces—from a dental clinic in South Mumbai to an auto repair garage in Pune, or a trendy hair salon in Bangalore—business owners face the ultimate test of poise: "Bhaiya, wahan toh yeh ₹500 sasta mil raha hai." (Brother, I'm getting this ₹500 cheaper over there.)

In the hyper-competitive Indian market, price comparisons are a daily ritual. Whether you are running a pharmacy in Delhi, a boutique hotel in Jaipur, or a local restaurant in Chennai, hearing that a competitor is undercutting your prices can trigger an instant defensive reaction. Your heart rate spikes, your margin anxiety kicks in, and you are tempted to either slash your prices defensively or fire back a rude dismissal.

Stop. Take a deep breath. Hearing that a customer found a cheaper alternative is not a rejection of your business; it is an invitation to demonstrate your superior value. Handled correctly, you won't just close the sale—you’ll earn a loyal patron who leaves a glowing 5-star review, which you can easily capture using automated tools like ReviewBoostPRO right after the transaction.

Why Indian Customers Say "It's Cheaper Elsewhere"

Before crafting your response, it is crucial to understand the psychology behind the statement. In India, bargaining and value-seeking are deeply ingrained cultural habits. When a customer says they found a lower price, they usually fall into one of three distinct categories:

  • The Bargain Hunter: They want a discount simply because it’s expected. They might buy from you anyway if you hold your ground gracefully.
  • The Genuine Researcher: They have genuinely cross-shopped online aggregators, local grey-market vendors, or roadside competitors and want to ensure they aren't overpaying.
  • The Quality Skeptic: They like your shop, your hygiene, or your service, but they need reassurance that paying the extra ₹200 or ₹2,000 is actually worth it.

Panicking and offering an immediate 20% discount devalues your brand. If you are known as the cheapest option, you attract price-sensitive customers who are notoriously hard to please and rarely leave good reviews. Instead, you want to position your business as the premier choice.

The Danger of Race-to-the-Bottom Pricing If you match every lowball price thrown at you in cities like Hyderabad or Ahmedabad, you will eventually compromise on product quality, staff wages, or service standards. Protect your margins by selling value, not discounts.

The 4-Step Framework to Handle Price Objections

When a customer drops the price bomb on you, follow this proven four-step conversational framework to steer the dialogue away from mere numbers and back to value.

1. Validate and Acknowledge (Never Get Defensive)

Never respond with hostility or act insulted. Acknowledge their findings with calm confidence. For example: "I completely understand, sir. There are many options in the market today, and keeping an eye on your budget is smart." This disarms the customer immediately because they expect you to argue.

2. Uncover the Hidden Details (Apples to Oranges)

More often than not, the cheaper alternative is not an exact match. Gently probe into what the other quote includes:

  • For a clinic: Is it using genuine imported implants or cheaper local alternatives? Does the quote include follow-up consultations?
  • For an auto repair shop: Are they using genuine OEM spare parts or recycled second-hand parts?
  • For a salon: Are they using top-tier international hair color brands or unbranded local tubes that damage hair?

3. Highlight Your Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

Remind them of what they get with you that they won't get at the bargain counter. This could be your warranty, your expert technicians, your pristine hygiene standards, or your after-sales support.

4. Offer a Smart Compromise (If Necessary)

If you genuinely want to close the deal, never give a flat cash discount. Instead, offer a value-add. Throw in a free accessory, a complimentary service session, or extended warranty support. It protects your price integrity while giving the customer a sweet deal.

Ready-to-Use Scripts for Indian Business Owners

Here are word-for-word scripts you can use directly in your shop, clinic, or over WhatsApp chats with clients in Bangalore, Delhi, Mumbai, and beyond.

Script 1: For Service Businesses (Salons, Spas, Clinics)

Customer: "Your full-head global hair color costs ₹4,500. The salon near my lane in Bandra is charging only ₹2,800."

Your Reply: "I completely understand, ma'am. There are definitely salons offering lower rates. However, at our studio, we strictly use 100% ammonia-free professional products imported directly from Italy, and our senior stylists have over 8 years of experience. Cheaper alternatives often use harsh chemical developers that cause hair breakage and fading within two weeks. We want to ensure your hair stays healthy and glossy. Would you like me to include a complimentary deep-conditioning protein mask worth ₹800 with this session today?"

Script 2: For Retail & Pharmacies

Customer: "This organic cold-pressed oil is ₹1,100 here. Online discount stores are selling it for ₹850."

Your Reply: "Sir, online platforms are great for mass-produced items, but counterfeit batches and near-expiry stock are common issues. Every bottle on our shelf is sourced directly from the authorized factory distributor in Pune with a fresh batch manufacturing date. Plus, if you buy it here, you get it instantly without waiting 3 days, and if there's ever any issue, you can walk right back into our store for an immediate replacement. That peace of mind is worth the small difference."

Script 3: For Auto Repair & Workshops

Customer: "Your servicing quote is ₹6,500. The local mechanic near the highway said he’ll do it in ₹4,000."

Your Reply: "Boss, the roadside mechanic will use unbranded engine oil and local filters that can choke your engine over time. Our ₹6,500 package includes genuine OEM oil, computer diagnostic scanning, brake cleaning, and a 60-day service warranty. Furthermore, we record a video inspection of every part we check so you have 100% transparency. Do you want to risk your engine life to save ₹2,500?"
