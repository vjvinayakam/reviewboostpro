What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Says They Were Promised a Refund?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this scenario: You open your Google Business Profile dashboard on a busy Tuesday morning at your dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai. Your heart sinks. A 1-star review stares back at you from a patient named Rajesh Kumar, claiming: "Terrible experience. The root canal procedure was painful, and when I complained, the receptionist promised a full refund of ₹5,500. Now management is refusing to pay!"

Panic sets in. You immediately ask your front desk staff, who look blankly at you and swear they never made any such promise. Meanwhile, prospective patients across Mumbai are reading this accusation. In the high-stakes world of local Indian commerce—whether you run a trendy salon in Koramangala, Bangalore, a diagnostic lab in Hyderabad, or an auto repair workshop in Pune—handling refund claims incorrectly can severely damage your local brand trust.

So, how do you handle this ticking time bomb? Let’s break down the exact strategies, psychological frameworks, and ready-to-use reply templates to protect your business reputation.

Why Do Customers Claim Promises That Were Never Made?

Before firing off an angry defensive response on Google, it helps to understand why customers leave these types of reviews. Rarely is it an outright malicious lie (though bad actors do exist). More often, it stems from three common communication breakdowns:

  • The Misheard Process: A customer asked about a refund, and a staff member said, "We will look into your request and see if it qualifies," which the customer heard as a definitive "Yes."
  • Shift Changes and Miscommunication: Staff member Amit promised to check with the owner regarding a ₹1,200 bill dispute at your Chennai restaurant, but Amit left his shift without updating Priya, who handled the counter next.
  • Emotional Amplification: When frustrated, customers often exaggerate minor concessions or empathetic listening into official financial commitments.
⚠️ The Golden Rule of Public Replies Never argue back and forth on a public Google review. Your reply is not meant to win an argument with Rajesh; it is written for the hundreds of prospective local customers who will read your exchange tomorrow to judge your professionalism.

Step 1: Investigate Internally Before Typing a Word

Before responding publicly or reaching out via WhatsApp, pause. Pull your staff into a quick meeting or check your internal chat logs. If your business uses automated customer communication workflows, check your chat history to see what was actually communicated.

Verify whether any part of your service delivery fell short. If an appliance broke down at your Pune service center or a doctor ran two hours late at your Delhi clinic, acknowledge that friction internally. It gives you context on why the customer is so upset.

Step 2: Take the Conversation Off Google Immediately

Public shouting matches destroy local business credibility. Your first priority in any refund dispute review is to pivot the conversation to a private, controlled channel—ideally WhatsApp, where you can verify phone numbers, check invoices, and resolve the issue discreetly.

Here are three battle-tested templates you can adapt based on your specific situation:

Template A: Professional & Fact-Finding (Best for unclear situations)

"Hello Mr. Kumar, we take feedback very seriously at our clinic. We want to look into this immediately as patient satisfaction is our top priority. We checked with our front desk team regarding any committed refund, but we want to clarify the details directly with you. Could you please WhatsApp us at [Your Business WhatsApp Number] with your appointment receipt so our manager can personally sort this out for you today?"

Template B: Empathetic & De-escalating (Best for genuine service lapses)

"Hi Priya, we are truly sorry that your salon experience did not meet expectations. We strive for excellence at our Bangalore studio, and it sounds like there has been a breakdown in our communication regarding your bill. Please connect with our operations head directly on WhatsApp at [Number] so we can review your invoice and resolve this matter fairly."

Template C: Firm & Policy-Oriented (When clear company policy was broken)

"Hello Vikram, thank you for bringing this to our attention. Our standard refund policy requires manager authorization, which was unfortunately not recorded in this case. However, we want to make things right. Please share your billing details with our team on WhatsApp at [Number] so we can review the specifics of your visit and find a mutually respectful solution."

Step 4: Resolve Offline and Turn the Review Around

Once the customer messages you on WhatsApp, handle the dispute with empathy and commercial common sense. If a genuine mistake was made by your team—for instance, a receptionist mistakenly offered a partial refund—honor it. Goodwill is worth far more than a disputed ₹1,000 fee.

Once the refund is processed or the dispute is amicably settled, politely ask the customer if they would update their review to reflect the resolution. Many happy Indian business owners using ReviewBoostPRO find that customers who felt heard and respected online are often eager to update their 1-star rant into a fair 4-star or 5-star review praising prompt customer service.

Even if they simply delete the negative review, your Google rating is protected, and your local search ranking remains strong.

Summary Checklist for Business Owners

  • Stay Calm: Do not post an emotional or defensive reply attacking the customer's character.
  • Investigate Internally: Speak with your staff and review chat logs or billing records before replying.
  • Pivot to WhatsApp: Move the conversation out of the public Google review section into a private chat.
  • Honor Mistakes Gracefully: If your team caused confusion, own it and resolve it swiftly.
