What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Makes False Claims in a Google Review?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 24, 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this scenario: You open your Google Business Profile dashboard on a busy Tuesday morning at your clinic in Mumbai or your restaurant in Bangalore. Your heart sinks. A 1-star review is sitting right at the top. The user claims something completely fabricated—maybe that your food gave them severe food poisoning, or that your dental clinic charged them ₹5,000 extra for an unnecessary procedure, or that your auto repair shop in Pune replaced parts that were perfectly fine.

You know for a fact it never happened. Your blood boils. Your immediate impulse is to type out an angry, defensive response exposing the reviewer as a liar. Take a deep breath. Pause. Typing out an emotional outburst is the absolute worst thing you can do for your local business.

In India’s competitive local business landscape, prospective customers don’t just read reviews to see if you're perfect; they read them to see how you handle adversity and false allegations. When handled strategically, a fake review can actually become an asset that highlights your professionalism, courtesy, and integrity.

Why False Claims Hurt Local Businesses in India

Local businesses rely heavily on trust and word-of-mouth. Whether you run a pharmacy in Delhi, a salon in Chennai, or a boutique hotel in Hyderabad, your Google Business Profile is your storefront window. When malicious competitors, disgruntled ex-employees, or unreasonable walk-ins drop fraudulent 1-star reviews, it directly impacts your walk-in traffic and local SEO rankings.

Prospective customers skim through reviews in seconds. If they see a horrifying claim about hygiene or overcharging without any pushback or explanation from your end, they assume it is true and take their business to your competitor down the road.

⚠️ The Golden Rule of Review Management Never argue, insult, or lose your temper in a public review response. Remember: you are not writing your reply for the dishonest reviewer. You are writing your reply for the hundreds of prospective future customers who will read this thread tomorrow.

Step 1: Check the Facts and Identify the Motive

Before typing a single word, investigate your internal records. Look up your patient logs, invoice databases, appointment registries, or CCTV footage if necessary. Ask your staff members who were on duty during the alleged incident.

Often, you will find one of three things:

  • A genuine case of mistaken identity: The customer visited a similarly named establishment in another locality and accidentally reviewed your store.
  • A competitor tactic: A fake profile with zero other reviews leaving a vague, damaging statement.
  • A miscommunication: A customer who misunderstood your billing structure, cancellation policy, or treatment timeline.

Step 2: Follow the "PAUSE" Framework for Your Response

When crafting your reply to a false claim, use a structured, calm framework designed to de-escalate and show future customers that you care:

  • Professional Greeting: Address the reviewer politely. If their name is a pseudonym or completely unrecognisable in your records, acknowledge it neutrally.
  • State the Facts Gently: Politely clarify that you have checked your logs and have no record of this specific incident, transaction, or visit under their name or details.
  • Take It Offline Immediately: Provide a direct phone number, email address, or WhatsApp contact link so they can share their actual bill or invoice details to investigate further.

Example Templates for Different Scenarios

Here are three ready-to-use templates tailored for Indian business owners dealing with false claims:

Template 1: For completely fake/unverified claims
"Hello [Name], we take all feedback extremely seriously at our clinic, but after thoroughly checking our patient records and appointment logs for the past month, we cannot find any record of a visit under your name or details. We pride ourselves on transparent pricing and patient care. Could you please reach out to our clinic manager directly at [Phone Number] with your appointment receipt so we can investigate this immediately?"

Template 2: For suspected mistaken identity
"Hello [Name], thank you for sharing your thoughts. However, we believe there might be a mix-up regarding the establishment you visited, as our restaurant does not serve the items mentioned in your review, nor do we have any table reservation matching your profile from this week. Please feel free to contact us at [Phone Number] if you believe you have reviewed the wrong outlet, and we would be happy to help!"

Step 3: Report and Flag the Review to Google

Don't rely solely on your reply. Google has strict content policies regarding fake reviews, spam, conflicts of interest, and defamatory language. Take these administrative steps:

  • Click on the three dots next to the review on your Google Business Profile dashboard.
  • Select "Report review".
  • Choose the violation category (e.g., Spam, Misrepresentation, or Conflict of Interest).
  • Track your request status in the Google Review Management Tool. If Google refuses to remove it initially, having a calm, professional reply already posted protects your reputation in the interim.

Step 4: Drown Out False Claims with a Flood of Authentic 5-Star Reviews

The absolute best medicine for a malicious 1-star fake review is an overwhelming wave of authentic, glowing 5-star reviews from your actual, happy customers. One isolated 1-star review surrounded by forty glowing 5-star reviews posted over the last two weeks loses all its power. Prospective customers instantly recognize it as an outlier.

However, getting customers to leave reviews manually is notoriously difficult. Indian consumers are busy; even when they love your service, they forget to leave feedback once they walk out your door.
