10 Google Review Request Templates for Indian Businesses

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

If you run a local business in India—whether it is a dental clinic in Mumbai, a trendy salon in Bandra, a multi-cuisine restaurant in Koramangala Bangalore, or an auto repair workshop in Pune—you already know how vital Google Business Profile reviews are. When a prospective customer searches for "best dentist near me" or "top car mechanic in Delhi," Google's local pack algorithm heavily favors businesses with a high volume of recent, 5-star ratings.

Yet, asking for a review can feel awkward. If you send a generic email, it goes straight to the promotions tab and gets ignored. SMS marketing often feels intrusive or gets blocked by DND (Do Not Disturb) registries. This is precisely why WhatsApp has become the undisputed channel for Indian business communication. With over 500 million active users in India, WhatsApp messages boast an open rate of over 98%.

To help you turn happy walk-ins and satisfied patients into glowing Google reviews, we have curated 10 ready-to-use WhatsApp templates tailored specifically for Indian local businesses. Let's dive in.

1. The Warm & Personal Clinic / Healthcare Template

Patients visiting a doctor, pediatrician, or dental clinic value empathy and personal care above all else. Your review request should reflect that warmth while making it effortless for them to share feedback right from their smartphones.

Template #1: Healthcare & Dental "Namaste Dr. Sharma here from CarePlus Clinic, Mumbai 🙏 Thank you for visiting us today. We truly hope you are feeling better. If you were happy with your consultation, could you please take 30 seconds to drop us a quick Google review? It helps other families in our neighborhood find trusted care: [Google Review Link]"

2. The Restaurant & Cafe Feedback Template

In the bustling Indian food scene—from South Indian tiffin centers in Chennai to upscale cafes in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad—word of mouth is everything. The best time to ask for a review is right after a satisfying meal or via a quick follow-up message while the flavors are still fresh in their minds.

Template #2: Restaurants & Cafes "Hi Rahul! Thank you for dining with us at Spice & Sizzle, Delhi tonight. We hope you loved the butter chicken! 🍛 If you enjoyed your experience, we'd love your support on Google. It only takes a tap: [Google Review Link]. See you again soon!"

3. The Salon, Spa, & Grooming Template

Clients leave hair salons, spas, and aesthetic clinics feeling pampered, refreshed, and looking their absolute best. Capitalize on that post-pampering dopamine rush with a polite, image-conscious WhatsApp ping.

Template #3: Salons & Spas "Hi Priya! ✨ Loving your fresh new haircut? We certainly loved having you at Glow Studio, Bangalore today. If you enjoyed our service, would you mind sharing your experience on Google? It means the world to our small styling team: [Google Review Link]"

4. The Auto Repair & Car Service Workshop Template

Car and bike owners in India are fiercely protective of their vehicles. When they find an honest mechanic who doesn't overcharge for spare parts, they are more than willing to leave a review—provided you remind them as they pick up their vehicle.

Template #4: Auto Repair Workshops "Hi Amit, your Swift is all serviced, washed, and ready for pickup at Apex Motors, Pune! 🚗🔧 We hope it drives like a charm. If our service met your expectations, please leave us a 5-star Google review here: [Google Review Link]. Drive safely!"

5. The Boutique Hotel & Homestay Template

Whether tourists are booking a heritage stay in Jaipur or a business hotel in Ahmedabad, reviews dictate booking decisions on platforms like Google Maps and OTA sites. Send this template on the morning of their check-out.

Template #5: Hotels & Homestays "Dear Vikram, thank you for staying with us at Heritage Palms, Jaipur. We hope you had a comfortable and royal stay! As a local independent property, your review helps us immensely. Could you leave us a note on Google? [Google Review Link]. Have a safe journey ahead!"

6. The Pharmacy & Wellness Store Template

Local chemist shops and neighborhood pharmacies build deep trust with regular elderly patrons and families. A friendly reminder via WhatsApp bridges the digital gap for local retail businesses.

Template #6: Pharmacies & Retail "Hello aunty, this is Verma Ji from Verma Chemists, Kolkata. Hope your medicines are working well. We strive to provide genuine medicines and home delivery to our neighborhood. If you appreciate our service, please support us with a Google review: [Google Review Link] 🙏"

7. The Direct & Short Template (For Busy Professionals)

Sometimes, less is more. If your customer base is fast-paced (think tech professionals in Bangalore or finance executives in Mumbai BKC), cut straight to the chase with an ultra-short request.

Template #7: Short & Direct "Hi Sneha! Thanks for choosing UrbanEdge Studio today. If you loved our work, a quick 5-star Google review would really help our small business grow: [Google Review Link] Thank you so much! 🙌"

8. The Milestone / Anniversary Gratitude Template

Build long-term brand loyalty by connecting your review request to a milestone—such as completing one year as a customer, or celebrating your business's local anniversary.

Template #8: Milestone Appreciation "Hi Manoj, it’s been absolute pleasure serving your family at FitnessFirst Gym, Chennai over the past 6 months! 💪 If we’ve helped you get closer to your fitness goals, would you take a moment to leave us a Google review? [Google Review Link]. Keep crushing it!"

9. The Referral & Review Combo Template

Combine review generation with local word-of-mouth referral incentives (where permitted by your industry ethics, such as loyalty discounts on the next bill).

Template #9: Incentive / Loyalty Combo "Hi Rohan, thank you for shopping at Metro Electronics, Hyderabad! 📱 As a token of thanks, here is a 10% off coupon for your next accessories purchase. Also, if you loved our store, help us spread the word with a Google review: [Google Review Link]"

10. The Gentle Follow-Up Template (For Unanswered Requests)

Customers get busy. The first WhatsApp message might get buried under work chats and family groups. A polite, non-pushy follow-up 48 hours later often secures the review.

Template #10: Gentle Follow-Up (48 Hours Later) "Hi Ananya! Just floating this to the top of your chat window. 😊 We hope you loved your dental checkup at SmileCraft, Delhi. Whenever you have 30 seconds free today, we would be thrilled to hear your feedback on Google: [Google Review Link]. Have a wonderful week!"

Best Practices for Asking for Google Reviews in India

To maximize your response rates when deploying these templates across Indian markets, keep these golden rules in mind:

  • Timing is Everything: Send the message within 1 to 3 hours of service completion while the customer's experience is fresh.
  • Always Use WhatsApp: Avoid traditional SMS or cold emails. WhatsApp feels personal, trusted, and frictionless in India.
  • Keep Links Direct: Never make customers search for your business on Google Maps. Provide a direct, short link that opens the review dialog instantly.
  • Never Incentivize Fake 5-Star Reviews: Google's algorithm detects suspicious review patterns. Focus on genuine customer satisfaction and excellent service quality.
