How to Respond to an Unfair Google Review: A Guide for Indian Businesses

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this: It is a busy Saturday evening at your family restaurant in Bandra, Mumbai, or your multi-speciality dental clinic in Anna Nagar, Chennai. Your team is working tirelessly to keep customers happy. Later that night, you check your Google Business Profile and your heart sinks. A fresh 1-star rating stares back at you, accompanied by a review claiming horrible service, rude staff, or a product that simply never existed.

For local business owners across India—whether running a bustling auto repair workshop in Pune, a high-end salon in Bangalore, or a neighborhood pharmacy in Delhi—receiving an unfair or completely fake negative review feels deeply personal. It can trigger panic, anger, and a desperate urge to fire back an angry defense.

Take a deep breath. How you respond to an unfair Google review in 2026 can either make your brand look mature, trusted, and professional, or petty and defensive. Because prospective customers read your replies just as carefully as they read the original review, your response is actually written for your future patrons, not the disgruntled (or fake) reviewer.

Why Unfair Reviews Hurt Local Indian Businesses

Consumer behavior in India has fundamentally shifted. Before stepping into a dental clinic, booking a hotel room in Hyderabad, or ordering from a new restaurant, checking Google Maps ratings is second nature. A sudden drop from 4.6 to 4.1 stars due to a malicious 1-star review can directly impact daily walk-ins and local trust.

Unfair reviews typically fall into three categories:

  • The Mistaken Identity Review: A customer meant to review the store next door, or confused your clinic with another medical practitioner down the street.
  • The Competitor Sabotage: Unscrupulous local competitors using fake accounts to drag down your ratings.
  • The Unreasonable Extortionist: Customers who demand free services, unreasonable discounts, or a full refund for a service delivered perfectly, threatening a 1-star review if they do not get their way.
The Golden Rule of Public Replies Never argue, use sarcasm, or get defensive in a public review response. Potential customers judge your business character entirely by how composed and gracious you remain under pressure.

Step 1: Pause, Breathe, and Investigate Internally

Your first instinct might be to hammer out a furious reply exposing the reviewer's lies. Resist this urge. Step away from your screen for at least an hour.

Once you are calm, investigate your internal records. Check your appointment software, billing logs, CCTV footage, or staff registers for the date and time mentioned. Ask your team members if they recall an interaction matching the description. Sometimes, a genuine misunderstanding occurred—perhaps a patient had to wait longer than usual because of an emergency surgery, or a courier delivery got delayed during heavy monsoon traffic in Mumbai.

Step 2: Try to Resolve Offline First

If the review points to a potential customer interaction, your response should invite them to sort out the issue privately. Provide a dedicated phone number or email address.

Example response template:

"Dear [Name], we take our service standards very seriously at our clinic in Bangalore, but we cannot locate any record of an appointment under your name or details. We want to understand what went wrong. Please connect directly with our clinic manager at [Phone Number/Email] so we can investigate this immediately."

Often, fake reviewers or bots will completely ignore offline follow-up attempts, which clearly signals to other prospective clients that the review was bogus to begin with.

Step 3: Flag and Report Violating Reviews to Google

If the review violates Google’s Content Policies—such as containing abusive language, spam, conflict of interest, or completely irrelevant content—you have the right to flag it.

To flag a review on your Google Business Profile:

  • Log into your Google Business Profile dashboard.
  • Navigate to the "Reviews" tab.
  • Find the unfair review, click the three vertical dots next to it, and select "Report review".
  • Choose the specific reason that best matches the violation (e.g., "Spam", "Off-topic", or "Conflict of interest").

Keep in mind that Google's automated moderation can be slow, and not every flagged review gets removed. If Google declines your removal request, your public reply remains your best shield.

Step 4: Write a Professional Public Response

If the review stays up, draft a polite, professional reply geared toward building trust with future customers reading your profile. Here is a winning framework to follow:

1. Acknowledge and Remain Polite: Start by addressing them respectfully.
2. State the Facts (Calmly): Clarify your policy or state that you have no record of their visit.
3. Offer a Resolution Channel: Provide a direct way to speak offline.

Sample Winning Reply for a Fake Review "Hello [Name], thank you for sharing your feedback. However, after checking our client logs and booking systems across our outlets, we have no record of your visit or service interaction. We pride ourselves on transparent, top-quality service. Please reach out to our management team at [Email/Phone] with your invoice or booking reference so we can verify and resolve this right away."

Step 5: Dilute the Impact by Collecting Fresh Positive Reviews

The absolute best antidote to an unfair 1-star review is a flood of genuine 5-star reviews from your happy, loyal customers. When prospective clients see one isolated negative review surrounded by dozens of recent, glowing testimonials praising your staff, food, clinical care, or craftsmanship, they will easily recognize the outlier as noise.
