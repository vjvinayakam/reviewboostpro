What to Do While Waiting for Google to Remove a Review

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 24, 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this: It is a busy Monday morning at your multi-specialty dental clinic in Andheri, Mumbai. While checking your WhatsApp notifications, you open your Google Business Profile manager and your heart skips a beat. A fresh 1-star rating sits right at the top, accompanied by a vicious, completely fabricated paragraph claiming a patient suffered severe nerve damage due to unhygienic equipment.

You know for a fact this person has never set foot in your clinic. Panic sets in immediately. You rush to the Google Business Profile help desk, click "Flag as inappropriate," and submit your formal removal request citing defamation and policy violations.

Then comes the agonizing part: The waiting game.

Google’s review moderation process can take anywhere from a few days to several frustrating weeks. Sometimes, automated filters reject your initial flag outright, forcing you to escalate the ticket through the Google Business Profile Help Community. In the meantime, every prospective patient, diner, or client searching for your business in Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, or Pune is scrolling past that devastating 1-star review.

How do you protect your hard-earned local reputation while your removal appeal languishes in Google’s queue? Let’s explore a practical, step-by-step game plan for Indian local businesses.

1. Write a Strategic, Professional Public Reply Immediately

Never leave a fake or malicious review unanswered. Leaving a 1-star review dangling without a response sends a silent signal to prospective customers that the accusation might be true. However, your response should not be an emotional outburst.

Remember: You are not writing this reply for the troll or spammer; you are writing it for the hundreds of genuine prospective customers who will read it later.

Keep your tone calm, objective, and professional. State clearly that your records show no such interaction. Invite the reviewer to contact your clinic or establishment directly with their patient ID or bill number to resolve the grievance. For instance:

"Hello. We take patient safety and service standards extremely seriously at our clinic. After thorough checking of our patient management system and appointment logs for the past 12 months, we have no record of any individual under this name or with these details visiting us. We request you to please contact our clinic manager directly at +91 XXXXX XXXXX so we can verify your visit and address any legitimate concerns."

This approach demonstrates to anyone reading the review that you are responsive, accountable, and confident in your service quality, while subtly signaling to casual readers that the review smells suspicious.

2. Drown Out the Negativity with an Instant Influx of 5-Star Reviews

Mathematics is your best friend when dealing with anomalous negative ratings. If your dental clinic or boutique restaurant in South Delhi has built up an immaculate 4.8-star rating across 320 happy reviews over three years, a single 1-star review will mathematically drag your overall score down, but only temporarily if you act fast.

The fastest way to neutralize a damaging fake review while waiting for Google's verdict is to bury it beneath a wave of authentic, glowing feedback from your actual, satisfied customers. This is where manual follow-ups fail because customers forget the moment they walk out your door. Smart business owners use automated tools like ReviewBoostPRO to trigger instant WhatsApp review requests the moment a customer pays their bill or finishes an appointment, making it frictionless for happy patrons to leave 5-star ratings within seconds.

Pro Tip for Local Businesses A sudden surge of 10 to 15 genuine 5-star reviews within 48 hours not only restores your high aggregate rating (pushing that 1-star review off the top fold of your profile), but it also signals to Google's algorithm that your business is vibrant, trusted, and actively serving local patrons.

3. Document Everything for the Appeal Escalation

If Google's automated system rejects your initial flag, do not throw your hands up in despair. You have a second line of defense: the Google Business Profile Help Community Forum.

To successfully get a Product Expert to escalate your case to Google engineering, you need concrete evidence. While you are waiting, gather:

  • Timestamped screenshots of the review showing the reviewer's profile name and account history (check if they have left 50 other 1-star reviews across different cities in India on the same day—a classic hallmark of bot networks or retaliatory local competitors).
  • Internal business records (appointment registers, billing software logs, guest reservation books) proving zero interaction with the named individual during the stated timeframe.
  • The Case ID provided in your email confirmation when you initially reported the review through the Business Profile Help tool.

4. Shift Customer Attention to Alternative Platforms

Depending on your industry, Google is not the only game in town. If you run a restaurant in Koramangala, Bangalore, diners check Zomato and Swiggy just as frequently as Google Maps. If you operate an upscale salon in Bandra, Mumbai, clients check your Instagram presence and Justdial listings.

While waiting for your Google listing to be scrubbed clean, double down on showcasing your incredible customer experiences on these secondary channels. Pin a story highlight on Instagram featuring real customer video testimonials, or share recent praise on your Facebook business page. Diversifying your digital footprint ensures that a temporary blemish on Google Maps doesn't choke off your entire stream of inquiries.

5. Keep Calm and Maintain High Operational Standards

The psychological toll of dealing with online defamation can spill over into your daily operations. Business owners often find themselves snapping at staff or becoming overly defensive with genuine customers because they are stressed about a malicious review.

Consciously resist this urge. Continue delivering the stellar service that earned you your loyal customer base in the first place. When new customers walk into your pharmacy, clinic, or hotel, treat them with warmth and exceptional care. Often, these very customers turn out to be your most passionate defenders who will gladly leave a detailed, glowing review when prompted via a quick automated WhatsApp nudge from ReviewBoostPRO.

What NOT to Do While Waiting Never engage in a public shouting match in the review comments. Do not threaten legal action in your public reply (it makes your business look hostile). And above all, never purchase cheap, shady fake review packages online to fight back—Google's spam detection algorithms will catch coordinated fake positive reviews instantly and may suspend your entire listing.

The Bottom Line

Dealing with fake Google reviews is an unfortunate rite of passage for growing local businesses across India. While you cannot control how fast Google's support team processes your removal ticket, you can control your reaction. By replying professionally, documenting evidence, and overwhelming the negativity with a steady stream of authentic customer praise powered by platforms like ReviewBoostPRO, you render malicious reviews powerless and protect your brand's reputation.
