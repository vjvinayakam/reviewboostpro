Step-by-Step: How to Find Your Google Business Profile Place ID

Before you can share a link, you need to identify your specific business listing on Google. If you haven't claimed your Google Business Profile (GBP) yet, head over to google.com/business and verify your location via postcard or phone call first. Once your listing is active, follow these steps to find your unique Place ID:

  • Step 1: Open your web browser and go to the Google Developers Place ID Finder tool (or simply search for "Google Place ID Finder").
  • Step 2: In the search bar on the map, type your exact business name and city (for example, "Gupta Dental Clinic Delhi" or "Sharma Auto Repairs Hyderabad").
  • Step 3: Click on your business name when it appears in the dropdown suggestion.
  • Step 4: A small information box will pop up on the map containing your business name, address, and a long alphanumeric code labeled Place ID.
  • Step 5: Copy this Place ID code and save it in a notepad. You will need it to construct your direct review URL.

How to Build Your Direct Review URL

Now that you have your Place ID, turning it into a clickable web link is straightforward. Google provides a standard URL structure that forces the review submission window to open.

Take your Place ID and paste it at the end of this base URL:

https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID

For instance, if your Place ID is ChIJN1t_tDeuEmsRUsoyG83frY4, your final review link will look like this:

https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJN1t_tDeuEmsRUsoyG83frY4

Alternative Method (Using Google Maps) You can also open Google Maps on your computer, search for your business, click the "Share" button, and copy the short link. However, this often gives a general map link rather than a direct pop-up review window. Using the Place ID method guarantees customers land straight on the rating screen.

Why Long Links Look Spammy (And How to Shorten Them)

Raw URLs containing Place IDs can look intimidatingly long and technical. If you send a messy link containing parameters and alphanumeric strings over WhatsApp to a customer in Chennai or Kolkata, they might hesitate to click it, suspecting spam or phishing.

This is why you should always use link shorteners (such as Bit.ly or your own custom domain) to transform your review link into something clean and trustworthy, like bit.ly/ReviewSharmaClinic or review.yourbrand.in.

To maximize your review generation campaign, keep these golden rules in mind:

  • Timing is Everything: Send the WhatsApp review request while the customer is still feeling the positive glow of your service—ideally within 30 minutes of leaving your store or clinic.
