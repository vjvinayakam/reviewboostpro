Should You Reply to a Review While It Is Being Reported?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial
• August 14, 2026 • 7 min read

Picture this scenario: It is a busy Saturday evening at your multi-specialty dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai, or your packed fine-dining restaurant in Indiranagar, Bangalore. You casually open your Google Business Profile dashboard, and your heart skips a beat. A fresh 1-star review pops up from a profile you have never seen before, claiming terrible service, rude staff, or worse, completely fabricated health code violations.

Your blood pressure spikes. You know instantly that this is a fake review—perhaps left by an aggressive competitor trying to siphon away your foot traffic, or a disgruntled former vendor holding a grudge. Your immediate instinct is to click the three dots next to the review, tap "Report review," and select the policy violation category. But then you freeze. The review is now sitting publicly on your profile while Google reviews your removal request. Should you leave a fiery public reply right now, or should you sit on your hands and wait?

This is one of the most common dilemmas faced by local business owners across Delhi, Chennai, Pune, and Hyderabad. Today, we are going to break down the exact mechanics of Google's review moderation process, analyze the psychological impact on prospective local customers, and give you a foolproof playbook on whether you should reply while a review is under dispute.

Understanding Google’s Review Removal Timeline

Before deciding on a public response strategy, you need to understand what happens behind the scenes when you report a review on your Google Business Profile. When you flag a review for violating Google’s content policies (such as spam, harassment, conflict of interest, or explicit content), it enters a queue.

For some businesses, automated filters catch and remove obvious spam within a few hours. However, for nuanced fake reviews—like a competitor writing a convincing fake complaint about your auto repair shop in Chennai—the review can linger for days or even weeks. Sometimes, Google’s automated system denies your initial report outright, forcing you to escalate the ticket through the Google Business Profile Help Community.

During this waiting period, prospective customers in your local area are actively looking at that 1-star rating. If your overall rating drops from 4.8 to 4.6 stars because of one malicious review, it can instantly impact your local pack rankings and conversion rates. This creates immense pressure to do something right away.

The Danger of Premature Action Many business owners make the mistake of emotionally lashing out in the comments the exact minute they report a review. This emotional reaction often plays right into the hands of a malicious reviewer or hurts your brand credibility more than the original fake rating.

The Case Against Replying Immediately: Why Silence Can Be Golden

Conventional wisdom in online reputation management usually dictates that you should reply to every single review, good or bad. However, when a review is actively being reported as fraudulent, there are compelling strategic reasons to pause and wait.

First, consider how Google’s algorithm and human moderators interpret engagement. If a review is reported for being spam or fake, and the business owner engages in a lengthy, heated argument in the comments thread, it signals to automated systems that the review is part of an active, authentic customer dispute rather than a clear-cut policy violation. While Google's official stance is that replies do not directly influence removal decisions, human reviewers looking at escalated tickets can sometimes be influenced by the tone and context of the interaction.

Second, if Google ultimately decides to delete the fake review after reviewing your report, any public reply you attached to it vanishes into thin air along with the review. If you spent twenty minutes drafting a professional rebuttal explaining why this person was never a customer at your Pune pharmacy, that effort is completely wasted once the review is purged.

The Case For Replying: Protecting Your Reputation in Real-Time

While waiting for Google to act is often the safest bet, there is a massive catch: Google can take anywhere from 48 hours to several weeks to process a removal request, or they might refuse to take it down at all.

During that multi-day window, hundreds of potential local customers will see that 1-star review sitting completely unanswered. To an outsider browsing your salon in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, a 1-star review with zero response from the owner looks damning. It creates the psychological impression that the accusation is true and that your business simply does not care about customer service.

Therefore, if your initial report does not result in an immediate deletion within 24 to 48 hours, you must assume the review is going to stick around for a while. At that exact juncture, drafting a calculated, calm, and professional public reply becomes non-negotiable. Your reply is not actually meant for the fake reviewer; it is written entirely for the prospective customers who are reading your profile before making a purchasing decision.

The Golden Rules of Replying While a Review Is Under Review

If you decide that you cannot afford to leave a suspicious review hanging without a response while your removal request is pending, you must follow strict communication guidelines. Never write out of anger.

  • Stay Calm and Objective: Never use accusatory language like "You are a fake account" or "Our competitors sent you." Even if you are 100% certain it is a competitor, sounding paranoid hurts your brand image.
  • Highlight the Discrepancy Politely: State clearly that your records do not show anyone under that name or with that specific experience visiting your establishment on the stated date.
  • Provide an Offline Resolution Channel: Invite the reviewer to contact your manager directly via a dedicated phone number or email address to verify their details.
  • Keep Future Prospects in Mind: Write your response as a public message reassuring casual readers that your business takes feedback seriously and investigates anomalies thoroughly.
Template for a Pending Review Response "Hello. We take all feedback extremely seriously at our clinic, but after thoroughly checking our patient records and appointment logs for the past 6 months, we have no record of an interaction matching your description. We want to investigate this immediately to ensure no mistakes were made—please reach out to our practice manager directly at support@yourdomain.com so we can verify your visit details."
