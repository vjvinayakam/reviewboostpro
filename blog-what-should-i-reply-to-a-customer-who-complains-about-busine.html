What Should I Reply to a Customer Who Complains About Business Hours?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Team
• August 2026 • 7 min read

Imagine this scenario: You run a popular dental clinic in Bandra, Mumbai, or a bustling family restaurant in Anna Nagar, Chennai. You have worked tirelessly for years to build a stellar local reputation. Then, a 1-star Google review pops up on your dashboard. The customer writes: "Reached at 8:30 PM expecting them to be open as per Google, but the shutters were down! Total waste of time and fuel."

Your blood pressure spikes. You know your official closing time is 8:00 PM, and Google Maps was simply displaying your standard hours while the customer arrived late, or perhaps it was a national holiday like Diwali or Independence Day when your hours changed. How you respond to this public grievance can make or break your local business brand.

In the fiercely competitive Indian local market, prospective customers read your review replies just as closely as they read the complaints themselves. Let's explore the step-by-step framework for handling business hour complaints professionally, keeping your local SEO strong, and turning potential PR disasters into wins.

1. Why Business Hour Complaints Damage Your Local Footfall

For an Indian consumer navigating heavy traffic in cities like Bangalore, Pune, or Hyderabad, making a trip to a pharmacy, salon, or auto repair shop requires deliberate planning and time investment. When they arrive only to find closed doors, their frustration is genuine. If left unaddressed or handled aggressively, a complaint about business hours signals to future clients that your business is unreliable or poorly managed.

Furthermore, Google's algorithm heavily factors in your local profile responsiveness. Leaving negative reviews unanswered hurts your overall store rating and visibility in local "near me" searches.

The Golden Rule of Review Replies Never argue, blame, or use an accusatory tone in public. Even if the customer made a mistake regarding your operational schedule, your reply should remain courteous, empathetic, and factual.

2. Scenario A: The Customer Arrived After Closing Time

This is the most common reason for hour-related complaints. The customer ignored your listed closing time or assumed you would stay open late "just in case."

How to reply: Acknowledge their effort in visiting your store, politely state your official operating hours, and invite them back during working hours.

Example Response:
"Hello Rajesh, thank you for sharing your feedback. We completely understand how frustrating it is to make the trip over from Andheri and find our clinic closed. For future reference, our standard OPD hours are Monday through Saturday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We would love to welcome you back during our working hours to assist you properly. Please feel free to call us at [Phone Number] before your next visit!"

3. Scenario B: Special Hours During Indian Festivals & Holidays

India's vibrant festival calendar—spanning Diwali, Eid, Christmas, Ganesh Chaturthi, and Pongal—often necessitates modified operating hours or unexpected closures. Customers accustomed to your regular schedule may get caught unaware.

How to reply: Politely remind them of the festive context, apologize for any inconvenience caused by the special schedule, and highlight where you publish these updates.

Example Response:
"Hi Priya, thank you for reaching out. We apologize that you found our salon closed on the evening of Dhanteras. As announced on our social channels, we had special festive timings that day to allow our staff to celebrate with their families. We are back to our normal hours (9 AM - 9 PM) now, and we hope you'll give us another chance to serve you soon!"

4. Scenario C: An Actual Error in Your Google Business Profile

Sometimes, the complaint is entirely justified. Perhaps your Google listing showed you open on Sundays when your physical store remains shut, or an accidental edit altered your closing time.

How to reply: Own the mistake immediately, thank the customer for bringing it to your attention, and confirm that it has been fixed right away.

Example Response:
"Dear Amit, thank you for bringing this to our notice. You were completely right—our Google profile incorrectly reflected our weekday hours for Sunday. We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience you faced traveling to our workshop. We have updated our listing immediately to prevent this from happening to anyone else. We hope you will drop by again soon."

5. Proactively Preventing Future Hour Complaints

Handling reviews gracefully is essential, but preventing the friction altogether is even better. Local business owners across India can take several tactical steps:

  • Update Special Hours Early: Use your Google Business Profile dashboard to update hours weeks in advance for national holidays and regional festivals.
  • Pin Announcements on WhatsApp: Keep your regular customers informed by updating your WhatsApp Business catalogue and status whenever your schedule shifts.
  • Maintain a Steady Stream of Positive Reviews: When a single negative review about hours pops up, having a robust pool of recent, glowing customer reviews dilutes its impact. 6. Moving the Conversation Offline

If a customer is extremely angry or claims they suffered a major financial loss due to a closed door, do not engage in a prolonged back-and-forth debate in the public review comments. Keep your public reply polite and invite them to connect privately.

By shifting the dialogue to a phone call or a direct WhatsApp chat, you demonstrate to all onlookers that you take customer service seriously while resolving the grievance in a controlled, personal environment.

Summary Checklist for Responding to Hour Complaints

  • Respond within 24 hours: Speed shows professionalism.
  • Keep it calm and courteous: Never lose your temper online.
  • State facts clearly: Mention your official timings without sounding defensive.
  • Correct the listing: If Google Maps was wrong, fix it instantly.
  • Keep collecting 5-star reviews: Overwhelm minor grievances with authentic praise from satisfied daily patrons.
