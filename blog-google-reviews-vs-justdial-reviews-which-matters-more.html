Google Reviews vs Justdial Reviews: Which Matters More for Indian Businesses?

RB
ReviewBoostPRO Editorial Team
• August 2026 • 9 min read

If you run a local business in India—whether it is a dental clinic in South Mumbai, a busy multi-cuisine restaurant in Koramangala, Bangalore, or an auto repair workshop in Pune—you have likely been caught in the crossfire of digital directories. For over two decades, Justdial was the undisputed titan of local search in India. Walk into any small business office across Delhi, Chennai, or Hyderabad, and you would find sales representatives from Justdial pitching listings, featured placement packages, and star ratings.

However, the landscape of Indian consumer behaviour has shifted dramatically. With smartphones in nearly every pocket and the near-universal dominance of Android and Google Maps, local discovery has undergone a tectonic revolution. Business owners are constantly asking: Should I invest my energy in Justdial or Google Business Profile? Do Justdial ratings still hold weight, or is Google the only game in town?

In this comprehensive guide, we will dissect the ecosystem of Google Reviews and Justdial Reviews, compare their impact on consumer trust and footfall, and help you decide where to direct your marketing efforts.

The Evolution of Local Search in India

To understand the debate between Google and Justdial, we must look at how Indians find services. Back in the 2000s and early 2010s, if someone needed a plumber, a diagnostic lab, or a wedding banquet hall, they dialled 8888888888 or opened the Justdial app or website. Justdial built an incredible directory backed by aggressive tele-sales teams.

However, Justdial is essentially a closed ecosystem. To see reviews or contact businesses, users need to interact with Justdial's specific properties. Meanwhile, Google integrated local search directly into the operating systems of Indian smartphones. When a customer in Andheri searches for "best dermatologist near me," Google Maps pops up instantly at the top of the search engine results page, complete with directions, photos, operating hours, and prominent star ratings.

The Shift in Consumer Intent Google handles over 95% of mobile searches in India. When consumers look for local businesses on Google, they are usually in immediate, high-intent mode—looking to visit, call, or book right then and there.

Comparing Google Reviews and Justdial Reviews

Let us break down the key differences between these two review platforms across critical business metrics:

  • Market Reach and Visibility: Google Reviews are visible to anyone using Google Search or Google Maps—which is virtually every internet user in India. Justdial reviews are restricted to Justdial's platform and app ecosystem. While Justdial still gets healthy traffic for certain B2B queries and service contractors, its general consumer reach is a fraction of Google's.
  • Trust and Authenticity: Consumers increasingly view Google Reviews as more organic and trustworthy. Because Google accounts are tied to personal Gmail profiles used for banking, YouTube, and daily communication, fake reviews on Google are slightly harder to scale without detection. Conversely, Justdial has historically battled perceptions of paid ratings and aggressive outbound sales calls where businesses could purchase enhanced rankings.
  • Local SEO Impact: This is where the gap is widest. Google uses the quantity, velocity, and quality of your Google Reviews as a direct ranking factor for the Google Local Pack (the top 3 map results). Justdial reviews have zero direct impact on your Google ranking. Winning on Google means you show up higher when local customers search.
  • Convenience of Review Generation: Getting customers to leave a review has always been the hardest part for Indian business owners. On Justdial, customers often have to log in or verify via OTP on a third-party app. On Google, customers can tap a direct short link, rate your business in three seconds, and walk away. Tools like ReviewBoostPRO streamline this exact friction point by sending automated WhatsApp messages with direct Google review links right after a customer's visit.

Where Justdial Still Holds Value

It would be unfair to write off Justdial completely. For specific categories in India, Justdial remains surprisingly resilient:

B2B services, wholesale suppliers, event planners, packers and movers, and industrial equipment dealers still receive valuable inquiries through Justdial. Many traditional Indian business owners report that Justdial leads, while sometimes requiring aggressive follow-up, tend to convert well because users on the platform are often comparing quotes across multiple vendors.

Furthermore, Justdial listings often rank well organically on Google for certain keyword combinations (e.g., "best catering services in Jaipur"). Therefore, having an active, claimed Justdial page with positive reviews doesn't hurt—it acts as an additional layer of digital presence.

The Verdict: Which Matters More?

If you are a local business owner with limited time and marketing budget, the answer is definitive: Google Reviews matter infinitely more than Justdial Reviews.

Here is why Google wins across the board:

  • Zero Platform Fees: You do not need to pay a subscription fee to maintain a top-tier Google Business Profile or to collect Google Reviews. Justdial frequently pressures businesses into paid plans to suppress negative reviews or boost visibility.
  • Direct Action Integration: Google lets users call you, chat with you, get live GPS directions, and view your website with one tap.
  • Global Standard: Whether a customer is a local resident or an NRI visiting family in Hyderabad, they use Google Maps to navigate and evaluate services. Nobody downloads Justdial when visiting from abroad.
The Danger of Neglecting Google If your competitor down the street has 300 five-star Google reviews and you have 12, Google Maps will push them to the top of the screen. In modern retail and healthcare, being out of the top 3 on Google Maps means losing up to 70% of potential walk-in customers before they even know you exist.
